How it works

For small service businesses that pay dozens of freelance suppliers from chat messages. Every request travels one line through five stations.

ReadClaude reads the request and its job sheet, invoice or photo.
VerifiedFixed rules check it against the job, the rate card and past payments.
RoutedEvery rail is quoted on what the supplier receives.
PaidThe rules run again, then your own account pays.
ReconciledSettlement is confirmed and lands in your books.

The safety promise

US bill-pay tools assume a purchase order. Checkrail works from job sheets, bookings and rate cards.

The model never holds the keys.

Claude can read and advise. It has no tool that moves money. Deterministic rules decide, and you set the limits.

Never holds your money.

Payments go from your own PayPal, Wise or bank account straight to the supplier. Checkrail only sends the instruction.
