One line from request to books

For small service businesses that pay dozens of drivers, guides and homestays from chat messages, invoices and trip sheets. Every request travels the same five stations.

  1. ReadClaude reads the request and its trip sheet, invoice or photo.
  2. CheckedFixed rules check it against the job, the rate card, past payments and PayPal's own records.
  3. RoutedEach rail is quoted on what the supplier actually receives.
  4. PaidThe rules run again, then your own account pays, inside your caps.
  5. ReconciledSettlement is confirmed and lands in your books and Xero.

Agents are starting to pay people. Checkrail is what they ask first.

Checkrail is an MCP server. Claude Desktop, Claude Code or a booking system's own agent calls it before paying a supplier, gets pay, hold or block with the reason, and can hand the payment over. Your rules and your autonomy setting decide. The agent never holds the keys.

Trusted in a week, starting from your own history

The safety promise

US bill-pay tools assume a purchase order. Checkrail works from job sheets, bookings and rate cards, and keeps the model away from the money.

The model never holds the keys.

Claude reads and advises. It has no tool that moves money. Fixed rules decide, and you set the limits.

Never holds your money.

Payments go from your own PayPal, Wise or bank account straight to the supplier. Checkrail only sends the instruction.
