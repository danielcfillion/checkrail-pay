# Checkrail Pay design review, round 7 (track C, cycle 2 re-test)

Reviewer: the same independent design reviewer as round 6. Checkout `review-c2` at commit 54e17d0, compared with 43b40a6. Design only.

## Bottom line

The fixer closed most of round 6 and, importantly, closed it properly: the Daily close overflow, the Vietnamese onboarding page and the approval band are all measurably better. One Must-fix remains, and it is the same defect as DES-1 in a different state. The approval band no longer says "Every check passed" over a reader concern, but the Ready to pay band still says "All 11 checks passed" next to a Pay with PayPal button while the agent panel shows 25% confidence and a possible duplicate. That is demo step 4, the "clean one". I am not satisfied until that is closed.

Nothing the fixer changed broke layout, contrast or overflow. I found two small regressions of its own making (a count that disagrees across pages, and a focus order that no longer matches the phone's visual order on Daily close) and a handful of nice-to-haves.

## How I re-tested

- Fresh database in `review-c2/data/`, `npm run build`, `next start` on port 3136, `CHECKRAIL_DEMO_PERSONAS=1`, one admin login, mock rails. Server pid 29270 (a separate pid file is in `c2-work/server.pid`); stopped by that pid at the end.
- Plain 390x844 and 1440x900 viewports (no touch emulation for overflow; touch emulation only for tap sizes), light and dark. 31 paths x 2 themes x 2 widths = 124 measurements, run twice: on the fresh seed, and again after I had approved Dao, paid Maria's USD 180, and created five extra requests, so `/close` had a "Sent by people" row.
- Rendered contrast walk (every text node, real background compositing) on 24 pages per theme, including the onboarding, remit and evidence pages.
- Vietnamese onboarding re-run through the real invite flow (AP clerk persona), both widths, both themes, every error state.
- Approval band wording read in every state I could produce through the UI. Inbox default checked for owner, approver, clerk and accountant.
- Keyboard tab order on the approval page, Daily close (desktop and phone) and overview; reduced motion; the 401 page; the loading skeleton (by delaying the navigation request); the remit and evidence pages.

## Regression hunt

**Band wording (`check-statement.ts`).** Read in the UI: clear (Linh 92%, Dao 93%: "Every check passed", true), reader concern on a recorded run (Ana: correct, attributed to "the recorded Claude run", says the rules did not see it), blocked (Phuong, a duplicate: heading and reason agree, with "Policy also found 1 more block and 3 warnings"), one warning (a clerk's view of an operator-supplied record: "Needs your approval: 1 warning on USD 420.00"). Low confidence could not be produced: the rule-based reader returned 98%, 95%, 85% for my requests, and 70% only on a needs-evidence request, so that branch is checked from the code and its unit test only. Reading `firstClause`, a concern is cut at the first colon or full stop, so Ana's concern loses "status P (pending). Confirm whether it completed or failed"; what remains is still a faithful headline and the full text is in the panel below, so I do not call it untrue. The one place it can still say something untrue is NEW-1.

**Inbox default.** Fine for every role (above). Edge: after an approver clears their last item the default silently becomes All, which is reasonable.

**Daily close reordering.** Works, with NEW-2 and NEW-3.

**Static 401 page.** Correct status and header, constant HTML, dark and light, phone and desktop, no overflow, claims nothing untrue.

**`<html lang>` script.** `document.documentElement.lang` is `vi` or `en` as asked, on the first paint and after the language link. Raw HTML says `en`. Acceptable.

**Focus order and screen-reader basics.** Skip link first on every page, 2 px focus ring on every stop I tabbed (about 30 per page). Approval page: Approve is the third stop in the main region, Reject is the seventh stop after it. Overview: "N requests need you" is the first main stop, the station links announce "Verified: 3 open, $1,595.20, 4 blocked, ...". One H1 and logical H2s on every changed page. Error summary on the onboarding form takes focus and is `role="alert"`. Loading skeleton announces "Loading the payment request".

**Dark mode.** No broken element on the changed pages: approval band with chip, onboarding error summary and success, Daily close, 401 page, skeleton, remit and evidence pages all read correctly.

## New findings

**NEW-1 (Must-fix). The Ready to pay band says "All 11 checks passed" over an open reader concern, at the button that moves money.**
- Page: `/obligations/<Maria Santos JOB-HG4D-0921>` (demo step 4, "A clean one, paid over PayPal"), green "Ready to pay: USD 180.00" band, and the same string in the `approved` case of `decision-model.ts` (line 302).
- Reproduce: 1440 px, light, persona Minh or Lan, fresh seed, open Maria's USD 180 request. The band says "All 11 checks passed. Recipient gets PHP 10,931.31 by PayPal, fee USD 3.60." with "Pay with PayPal". The panel below shows "Claude's confidence 25%", "Possible duplicate: a PayPal transaction on 12 Oct 2026 ... already pays this job reference" and "payer_info shows a different counterparty (John Doe, Hanoi, VN)". The Policy panel was updated by the fixer ("Every rule passed. The agent's concerns are not rules"); the band was not.
- Why it matters: it is the same contradiction as DES-1, now on the pay click rather than the approve click, and on the demo's own "clean" story. A judge reading the page will see the product reassure and warn in the same view.
- Fix: run the `approved` case through `checkStatement` as the awaiting case does ("Rules passed (11 checks), but the recorded Claude run raised 2 concerns: Possible duplicate. Confidence 25%. The rules did not see this run."), add the chip, and use the same statement in the autopilot sentence at `obligations/[id]/page.tsx` line 320 ("Every check passed and this is within your caps...").

**NEW-2 (Should-fix). Four different counts for "needs you".** Overview "11 requests need you", sidebar and Inbox tab "4 waiting for you", Daily close "Waiting on you 7" (it includes three Needs evidence requests that wait on the vendor). On the fresh seed they are 6, 4 and 4. Fix: one definition for "waiting on you"; show the evidence ones as "waiting on the vendor" in their own line or panel.

**NEW-3 (Should-fix, small). Daily close focus order does not match the phone's visual order.** Phone tab order: Needs a look, then Sent by people (page y 1694), then back up to Still waiting on you (y 1177). The panels are reordered with CSS. Fix: put the DOM in the phone order and let the desktop grid place Sent by people in the left column.

**NEW-4 (Nice-to-have). A raw state code leaks into Daily close.** "Reference JOB-KAL2D-1002 was already used by an earlier payment (awaiting_approval)" where earlier text said "(settled)". Fix: humanise the state ("awaiting approval") in the duplicate reason.

**NEW-5 (Nice-to-have). The onboarding error mentions an account the form does not offer.** "Vui lòng điền ít nhất một cách nhận tiền: email PayPal, tài khoản Stellar hoặc số tài khoản ngân hàng." There is no Stellar field on the vendor form. Fix: name only PayPal email and bank account.

**NEW-6 (Nice-to-have). The remit and evidence vendor pages are English only, and the evidence intro is garbled** ("Ana Reyes, to pay USD 400.00 for your invoice, they need: ..."). The onboarding strings pattern would carry both. Fix: reuse it and rewrite the sentence ("To pay you USD 400.00 for this invoice, the business needs: ...").

**NEW-7 (Nice-to-have). One-off hydration error on `/close`.** See the sweep. Fix: render relative times only on the client, or add `suppressHydrationWarning` to the time element.

## Fixer decisions

1. **Not re-stepping amber and red by 15 L* (DES-9).** Acceptable for now. The palette in DESIGN.md is the product's identity, and every state carries a word and an icon (check, cross, clock), which is what WCAG asks for. The two places that relied on colour alone (toasts, changed payout values) are fixed. I would still re-step the palette before any wider release, because "Blocked" and "Awaiting approval" are indistinguishable in deuteranopia without reading the word. Cheap option that stays in palette: darken the amber to about #8a5200 (still above 4.5 on its tint) and lighten nothing else; check the pill tints in simulation after. Does not block.
2. **Not moving Reject and the note into the approval band (DES-4).** Acceptable, with a cheap improvement. The Approve button now says who and how much and that it does not send money, which was the substantive gap. Reject's distance is a speed issue, not a safety issue. Cheaper than restructuring `ActionBar`: add a "Reject" text link next to Approve that moves focus to the note field in Actions, and reword the line "The note goes with Approve above or Reject here" into "Add a note before Approve or Reject."
3. **Bank country and currency empty with "ví dụ: VN" and "ví dụ: VND" placeholders (DES-14).** Acceptable. Prefilling would store data the vendor did not give, the placeholders now read clearly as examples (screenshot read), and choosing a Vietnamese bank still fills both. A nicer pattern is to show those two fields only after an account number is typed.
4. **Trust sentence ("Chỉ Ridgeline Expeditions LLC xem được thông tin này, và số tài khoản ngân hàng được lưu dưới dạng mã hóa").** Acceptable. The wording is plain and short, and `render.yaml` sets `CHECKRAIL_DATA_KEY` for the hosted demo, so "encrypted" is true there. Local runs without the key use the public development key, which the server already logs. The Vietnamese still needs a native speaker's read, as the fixer notes; I read it as natural but I am not a native reviewer.

## Ratings

- Overall design: **8.0 / 10** (was 7.5). The system was already a 9; the remaining defect is a single, reproducible contradiction on the demo's step 4.
- Against the hackathon's design criterion: **8.5 / 10** (was 8). A judge now sees a Vietnamese page that stays Vietnamese, a phone that never scrolls sideways, a first-screen that leads with a decision, and a clear approve button. Closing NEW-1 is what would move this to 9.

## Am I satisfied?

**No.** One Must-fix is outstanding: NEW-1, the Ready to pay band that still says "All 11 checks passed" over a possible duplicate. It is a small change (the band already has the function it needs) and I would sign off as soon as it is made and the autopilot sentence at `obligations/[id]/page.tsx` line 320 gets the same treatment. DES-9 (not re-stepped), DES-4 (Reject distance) and DES-20 do not block me.
