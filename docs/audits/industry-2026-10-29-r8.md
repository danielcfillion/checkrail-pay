### Release and reader concerns on already-approved items

On the run page a Ready-to-pay line now carries "2 reader concerns, Low confidence 25%, Rules passed, but the recorded Claude run raised 2 concerns: Possible duplicate", and the confirmation says "2 going out carry a reader concern or low confidence that release does not stop for: read the lines first". Release then paid both, and also approved and paid Ana's waiting USD 750 that carried "appears to have already been paid, 30%".

Is that acceptable? For an item the approver has already approved, with the concern shown on the line and counted in the confirmation, yes: he has seen it, and a person who read it and pressed Release has decided. For a waiting item that the release itself approves, no. A bulk release should not be the approval of something the reader doubted. What I would want:

1. A waiting item with an open reader concern is left for its own page, exactly like one with a warning, and gets an Approve button on the run line that shows the concern (the pattern already exists).
2. For already-approved items, keep the count in the confirmation and add one tick, "I have read the 2 concerns", before Yes.

A caveat that matters: in live mode the agent's concerns become policy warnings (the `ai_flag` and low-confidence rules), so a live run would already skip such an item. The gap shows in the hosted demo, where the recorded reading is display-only ("The rules did not see this run"). It is still worth closing, because that is what judges will click.

## New findings

### Must-fix

None.

### Should-fix

**PR8-1. A stale approval is recorded, and the audit line reads like an approval.**
- *Did.* Minh opened the run page. Lan changed a waiting item's amount. Minh pressed Approve on the stale line.
- *Saw.* Nothing was approved in effect (the item was Blocked, then Awaiting approval again after Lan restored the amount), but the toast said "Approval recorded. Policy says: block." and the request's approvals showed "Approved by Minh", later "Rejected by Minh".
- *Why.* An auditor reading the trail sees an approval of a request the person never saw. The claim "an approval covers what the person saw" is true in effect but not in the record.
- *Fix.* Refuse with "This request changed since you loaded the page; reload and look again", record nothing, and let the audit line say "approval refused: changed".

**PR8-2. A release approves and pays a waiting item that carries a reader concern.** See the section above. *Fix:* treat a reader concern like a warning for waiting items (leave for its own page, Approve on the line) and add a "I have read the concerns" tick for approved ones.

### Nice-to-have

**PR8-3. Typo in the run confirmation:** "1 carryes a warning or only the vendor's own support". *Fix:* "1 carries".

**PR8-4. A blocked copy keeps its creation-time reason.** The second Rosa copy on "Waiting on you" still said "already used by an earlier request (ready to pay)" after the original had settled. *Fix:* refresh the reason text from the current state.

**PR8-5. PR7-7 and PR7-8 remain** (one transfer, two fees; bank confirmation is all-or-nothing). Both are small and neither blocks a pilot.
