# Checkrail Pay design review, round 6 (track C)

Reviewer: independent product and visual design reviewer (finance operations software, dense tables, accessibility). Commit 43b40a6, built and driven from my own checkout. Scope: design only, not security or accounting correctness.

## Bottom line

This is an unusually coherent, finished-looking product for a hackathon. The check line is a real organising idea, the token system is disciplined, rendered contrast is better than most commercial finance tools, keyboard focus is always visible, and the phone layout is largely a deliberate rework rather than a squeezed desktop. The approval card is the right idea: payee, amount, reason, delivered amount and one button, in one band.

Three things stop me calling it clean:

1. The approval screen can contradict itself. Ana Reyes's card says "Every check passed" in plain type while the agent panel two inches below shows "Claude's confidence 30%" and a possible duplicate payment. An approver in a hurry reads the card and clicks Approve.
2. The Daily close page overflows horizontally on a phone (content 420 px wide in a 390 px viewport) as soon as one payment has been sent. The demo's own story sends a payment (step 4) before it reaches Daily close (step 9), so a judge on a phone will hit it.
3. The Vietnamese onboarding page is Vietnamese only until something happens. Every validation error, the success confirmation, the expired link message, the page title, the skip link and the `lang` attribute are English, and errors arrive as eight second toasts detached from the field.

Fix those three and the rest is polish.

## How I tested

- Seeded a fresh database under the checkout's git-ignored `data/`, ran `npm run build`, started `next start` on port 3135 with `CHECKRAIL_DEMO_PERSONAS=1`, one admin login, mock rails. Stopped the server by pid when finished (pids 5949, 5948, 5936; I left another reviewer's server alone). `git status` is clean.
- Playwright (Chromium) at 1440x900 and 390x844, light and dark (`colorScheme` emulation: dark follows `prefers-color-scheme` only, there is no theme control, only the `data-theme` hook). About 55 page-state captures plus scripted flows: approve (desktop), pay (desktop), invite creation and onboarding submission (phone), keyboard tab order on the approval page and Daily close, reduced-motion emulation.
- Pages reached at 390 px in both themes (28 paths): overview, inbox (two filters), new request, five obligation states, exceptions, runs, vendors, vendor detail, new vendor, edit vendor, ledger, accounting, books, close, departures, audit, bank files, settings, demo, 404. Team lives inside Settings (there is no /team route).
- Contrast: a script walked every text node on 20 pages per theme, composited the real foreground (including opacity) over the real stacked background, and grouped by colour pair. 67 distinct combinations per theme. Token-level maths for non-text contrast and for colour-vision simulation (Machado 2009 matrices at full severity, CIE76 colour difference and CIE L* difference).
- Vietnamese onboarding: created tokens through the real UI (vendor page "Send them a link to update details", and /vendors/new "Make an onboarding link", as the AP clerk persona), then reviewed `?lang=vi` and `?lang=en`, both widths, light and dark, plus empty submit, name only, bad email, short account number, success, reused link and invalid token.

A caveat about my own method: in touch emulation Chrome widens the layout viewport to fit wide content, so `innerWidth` reports the wide number and a naive `scrollWidth > innerWidth` test passes. My first pass was blind to the Daily close overflow for that reason. The numbers in the overflow table come from a plain 390 px viewport with every disclosure opened.

## Findings

### Must-fix

**DES-1. Approval card says "Every check passed" while the agent panel flags a likely duplicate**
- Page and element: `/obligations/<Ana Reyes JOB-PAL3D-0927>`, the amber "Needs your approval: above USD 250" band versus the "What the agent read" panel.
- Reproduce: 1440 px, light or dark, persona Minh (approver). Open Ana's USD 750 request from Needs you. The band reads "Every check passed. Amount matches Trip sheet PAL-3D-0927." The middle column reads "Claude's confidence 30%", "so this may be a duplicate" and a Concerns list ("appears to have already been paid: PayPal transaction 878829956L370032P ... status P (pending)"). The Policy panel says "Every check passed. 11 checks passed." The only explanation is a small grey note that the reading is recorded and the decision comes from the rules. Phuong's card gets this right: the concern and the block agree.
- Why it matters: this is the one screen where a wrong click moves money. Two authoritative-looking voices disagree and the louder, higher one is the reassuring one. A judge who reads the middle column will think the product does not know its own state; an approver who does not will approve a possible duplicate. It undercuts the product claim.
- Fix: let the decision band carry the reader's output. When the reader's confidence is below a threshold or it lists concerns, change the sub-line to "Rules passed. The reader raised 1 concern: possible duplicate, PayPal shows USD 750 on 12 Oct (pending). Confidence 30%." and add a visible chip or count on the band ("1 reader concern"). If the recorded reading is meant to differ from this demo's rules, say so in the band ("Recorded live run disagrees with the rules in this demo, see below") rather than only in the panel header. Never print "Every check passed" without qualification when any reader concern is open.

**DES-2. Daily close overflows horizontally on a phone once a payment is listed**
- Page and element: `/close`, the "Sent by people" (and by the same markup, "Sent by autopilot") panel, a plain `<table>` with Paid to, Reference, Rail, Status, Fee, Amount columns, inside `div.mt-6.grid.gap-6.lg:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_340px]` (scrollWidth 404, clientWidth 358).
- Reproduce: 390x844 plain viewport (not touch emulation), light or dark. Seed, pay Maria Santos JOB-HG4D-0921 (demo step 4), open Daily close. `documentElement.scrollWidth` is 420 against `innerWidth` 390. Every panel is pushed about 30 px past the right edge and the 16 px gutter is lost. On a real phone this shows as a page that wobbles sideways and a zoomed-out view. With an empty period (the fresh seed) the page is fine, which is why a screenshot of the seed does not show it.
- Why it matters: the brief says the layout must never scroll horizontally, and Daily close is one of the two screens the product is judged on. It breaks on the demo's own path.
- Fix: below `lg`, make the grid a single `minmax(0,1fr)` column and give those panels `min-w-0 overflow-x-auto`, or better render each sent payment as the same two-line card the Inbox uses on phones (payee and amount on line one, reference, rail, status on line two). Add a Playwright check that opens `/close` at 390 after one payment.

**DES-3. Vietnamese onboarding turns English at the moments that matter**
- Page and element: `/onboard/<token>?lang=vi`, the form, its error and success states, the invalid-link page, and the document head.
- Reproduce: 390 px (also 1440), `?lang=vi`. Press "Gửi thông tin" with only a name: an English toast "Give at least one way to pay this vendor" (it addresses the business, not the person filling in the form). Enter a short account number: "Account number must be 6 to 20 digits". Submit a valid form: the form is replaced by "Thank you. Your details were sent. The business confirms them with you before the first payment." in English, under the unchanged Vietnamese heading that still asks for details. Open the used link again: "Link not available. This link has already been used." in English. Invalid token: "This link is not valid." in English. `<html lang="en">`, `<title>` "Your payment details", skip link "Skip to content" and the tiny "Sent with Checkrail Pay" are all English. Browser-native required and email messages follow the browser language, and a screen reader will pronounce the Vietnamese with an English voice.
- Errors are toasts: they auto-dismiss after 8 seconds, are not attached to the field, are not announced as errors (`role="status"`), and the toast stack is positioned 116 px above the bottom edge for the phone nav that does not exist on public pages. In my full-page capture they sit on top of the "Quốc gia của ngân hàng" and "Cách nhận tiền" fields.
- Why it matters: the Vietnamese page is the proof that the product fits its market. A vendor who cannot read English cannot recover from a mistake, and a trust-sensitive page (bank details) that switches language on confirmation reads as unfinished or unsafe.
- Fix: pass `lang` into the submit action and return message keys, not English strings; render an inline error summary above the button (`role="alert"`, focus moved to it) plus per-field messages; replace the form with a Vietnamese confirmation panel with a check icon and the reference; set `<html lang>` and `<title>` from the resolved language (public layout); localise the skip link and the invalid and used link pages (the token page already knows `lang`).

### Should-fix

**DES-4. The Approve button does not say what it will do, and Approve and Reject live in different places**
- `/obligations/<awaiting>`: Approve (band, top right) says only "Approve". What approving does ("Approving does not pay. Policy checks again, then the request is ready to pay.") is in 12 px grey text in the Actions column, below Reject. The note field that "goes with Approve above or Reject here" is in that column too. On a phone the sticky bar says "Needs your approval: above USD 250 [Approve]" with no payee and no amount.
- Fix: label it "Approve USD 750.00 to Ana Reyes" (the design principle in DESIGN.md says buttons name the action), put one line under it ("Does not send money. A person sends it next."), place Reject and the note beside it in the band, and give the sticky bar the payee and amount ("Ana Reyes, USD 750.00").

**DES-5. Overview buries the decisions and, on a phone, opens with chrome**
- `/` at 1440: the "Needs you" list starts about 1,250 px down, below the figures, the check line and a 30 day Money out chart and table. The line "6 requests need you." at the top is plain text, not a link. At 390: the first screen is the demo banner (three buttons including Reset demo), the Assist strip (two more buttons) and the top of the KPI card; the first money figure is at the fold, the Needs you list roughly 2,500 px down on a 3,700 px page.
- Fix: make "6 requests need you" a link to the Waiting for you filter; move Needs you above Money out (the check line hero can stay first); collapse the demo banner to one line on phones after the first view and keep Reset demo off the first screen (it is a destructive action with a two-step confirm on a shared demo).

**DES-6. Daily close ranks the wrong things**
- `/close`: the KPI row counts "Needs a look 2" but the four requests that need the approver (approvals and one send) sit in a narrower right column ("Still waiting on you 4"), and on a phone below two empty panels ("Autopilot is off, so the agent sent nothing", "Nobody sent a payment by hand"). "Sign off" is the top-right primary button on desktop and sits above the content on phone, before the person has read what they are attesting to. The label "Blocked by policy" and its left rule are amber, while blocked is red on every other page.
- Fix: add "Waiting on you" to the KPI row; on phone put Still waiting on you directly after Needs a look and collapse empty panels to one line; move Sign off to the end of the page (or repeat it there) with the confirm copy the button already has; use the blocked red for "Blocked by policy" and keep amber for things that wait on a person.

**DES-7. Inbox opens on "All 37" for an approver with 4 waiting**
- `/obligations`: the first tab is "Waiting for you 4" but the selected one is "All". Fix: default to Waiting for you when the count is above zero for the current role, otherwise All.

**DES-8. Phone tables hide the number that matters**
- `/books` at 390: the Aged payables table scrolls inside its panel and the Open (amount) column is cut off ("USD 18", "VND 10,80"), with no edge fade or hint; "not yet due" wraps to three lines. `/vendors` wraps money across lines ("USD" on one line, "1,750.00" on the next). `/departures` clips Services. Inbox, the one list that was redesigned as cards, shows what the others should do.
- Fix: nowrap on money cells; on phones render these tables as stacked rows (payee and amount first), or pin the amount column and add the fade used on the Settings tabs.

**DES-9. Some state still relies on colour alone, and the stepped colours only separate because of text and icons**
- Toasts (`Toast.tsx`): error versus success is a red or green 6 px dot, red border and red text; no icon or prefix; errors are `role="status"`, not `alert`. Vendors "Waiting for verification": the proposed values that changed (new PayPal email, "paypal" preference) are marked only by amber text. Money out bars are identified by the legend swatch only.
- Colour-vision check on the real tokens: in deuteranopia, amber against red is almost one colour in the light theme (colour difference 12, lightness difference 5), and green against red differs by lightness 2. In protanopia in the dark theme, green #3db58f against red #ec7a70 is 11 apart. Pills and banners survive because every one also carries a word and an icon (check, cross, clock), so D32 holds where text is present, but not for the three items above.
- Fix: add a check or cross icon and a visible "Error" prefix to toast tones, switch errors to `role="alert"`; add a "Changed" tag on proposed rows; step the lightness of attention versus blocked (for example a darker red or a lighter amber) so they differ by at least 15 L* in simulation.

**DES-10. Form fields fail non-text contrast, and placeholders are faint**
- Input and select borders use `--rule-strong` (ink-soft at 50%): 2.07:1 on white in light, 2.60:1 in dark, against the 3:1 needed for the boundary of a text input (WCAG 1.4.11). White fields on #edf0f2 paper are 1.1:1 so the border is the only edge. Placeholder text (`--ink-soft` at 80%) measures 3.59:1 in light, 4.52:1 in dark.
- Fix: field border a solid #7a8794 in light (3.67:1 on white, 3.2:1 on paper) and the matching lighter step in dark; placeholder at full `--ink-soft` (5.47:1).

**DES-11. Reduced motion is not honoured by the check line fill**
- `CheckLine.tsx` line 97 sets `transition: width 600ms ...` as an inline style on the progress fill. The `prefers-reduced-motion` rule in `globals.css` cannot override an inline style. With `reducedMotion: reduce` the computed duration is 0.6 s on the fill while the marker is 0 s. DESIGN.md and UI_NOTES say the pay animation is disabled for reduced motion; it is half disabled.
- Fix: move the transition into the `.dl-marker` class (or a new class) and remove the inline style.

**DES-12. Tap targets on touch devices**
- With touch emulation at 390, the check line hero dots on the overview are 16x16 and 18x18 links 20 px apart (named only by `title`). Names in the Daily close lists and vendor tables are 15 px tall links; the vendor link on the approval page is 31 px; the Inbox breadcrumb is 30 px wide. The rest (buttons, fields, rows, bottom nav, sticky Approve) meets 44 px thanks to the coarse pointer rules, which is good work.
- Fix: give those links the existing `.tap` padding or a pseudo-element hit area of 44x44, and group the hero dots into one tap target per station (the station summary link already exists).

**DES-13. A hosted-demo judge's first screen is a browser password prompt**
- Without credentials `/` returns a bare `text/plain` 401 "Authentication required", and the dialog is the browser's own. A judge who cancels, or opens the link in a preview, sees nothing about the product.
- Fix: return an HTML 401 body that carries the name, the one-line claim, and "The login is in the Devpost testing instructions", with the same `WWW-Authenticate` header.

**DES-14. Onboarding form clarity and trust**
- Two fields are both just "Email" and "Email PayPal" with no hint which is which. Only "Tên của bạn" is required and nothing marks it; one field says "không bắt buộc" (optional) instead. "Quốc gia của ngân hàng" and "Loại tiền" show VN and VND as grey placeholders, which read as filled values but are empty. No sentence says who can see the details, that they are stored encrypted, or that the business will phone to confirm. The bank list says "BIDV (BIDV)", "ACB (ACB)", "SHB (SHB)", "VIB (VIB)". Expiry reads "6/11/2026" with no month name. Fields are 32 px tall on desktop, fine for a mouse; at 390 they are 44 px.
- What is good: diacritics render correctly in Archivo (Hướng dẫn viên, Tài xế, Số tài khoản ngân hàng, Việt Nam all clean), labels are real `<label>`s with autocomplete tokens and `inputmode="numeric"` on the account number, single column on phone, 16 px gutter, no overflow in either language or theme.
- Fix: label the contact email "Email liên hệ", mark the required field, prefill VN and VND as real defaults (or selects), add one trust sentence under the heading, show the expiry as "6 tháng 11, 2026", drop the duplicated bank codes.

### Nice-to-have

- **DES-15. No `loading.tsx` or `error.tsx` anywhere.** Every page is dynamic. Navigation shows no progress, and a render error would show the framework default. Add a skeleton for obligation pages and a branded error page with a retry link. I did not trigger an error.
- **DES-16. Accounting table inconsistencies.** Amounts print without thousands separators (`1000.00`, `1020.00`) while Vendors prints `USD 1,750.00`; "Exported" is plain green text where every other status is a pill with an icon.
- **DES-17. `/demo` starts with "Ten steps, about ten minutes".** It has no one-line statement of the claim and no screenshot; the overview H1 is the business name, with the claim as grey subtitle. Lead the demo guide with "Checkrail Pay checks every supplier payment before money moves" and the 340 USD prevented figure. `/vendors/new` has the same document title as `/vendors`.
- **DES-18. Disabled primary buttons carry no reason.** "Verified, use these details" (2.0 to 3.0:1 at half opacity, exempt from WCAG but unexplained) and "Read from PayPal" should say what unlocks them ("Choose how you checked first").
- **DES-19. Small polish.** The Settings tab row's right-edge fade mask shows as a faint streak on desktop where nothing scrolls. The demo role select truncates "Minh (operations mana". The mobile sticky approve bar overlaps the bottom nav by 6 px (bar 728 to 793, nav from 787).
- **DES-20. Overview hero labels are 12 px grey on a hairline.** The check line is the product's one memorable device and on the overview it is the quietest thing on the page; consider making station labels and counts 14 px.

## What already works well

- The check line: one device, three scales (row, page header, overview hero), `role="img"` with a spoken label at every scale, a red cross where a request stopped, a caption saying why.
- The approval band's grammar: tinted band, icon plus heading plus one-sentence reason plus the delivered amount plus the one button; state change is obvious (Dao went from amber to green "Ready to pay: USD 425.00" with the next button). Blocked cards say what is wrong in the heading ("Blocked: USD 160 more than the trip sheet").
- Tokens: every colour is a custom property, dark is a one-step lightening, and the rendered contrast is excellent. Dark mode has no broken page, no unreadable element, no pale panel left over.
- Keyboard and screen reader basics: a working skip link first in tab order, a 2 px focus ring on every control (I tabbed 22 stops on two pages and all showed it), `aria-current` on the nav, `aria-pressed` on the date range control, a keyboard operable chart with an aria label that explains the keys, labelled landmarks (Sidebar, Main, Breadcrumb, Filter, Settings sections), one H1 and sensible H2s on every page, distinct page titles, labels on every input except the two date inputs on Books, no images without alt.
- Honesty: the sidebar footer always shows the rail and reader modes, the demo banner says the demo is shared and what Reset does, the recorded Claude reading is labelled as recorded.
- The phone layout: bottom nav with 57 px targets, sticky Approve bar, card-style Inbox, 44 px controls under the coarse pointer rule, 16 px gutters, 27 of 28 tested paths free of horizontal overflow (the one exception is DES-2).
- Empty and quiet states are written for people ("Autopilot is off, so the agent sent nothing", "No sign-offs yet. The first one starts the record", "No runs yet.").
- Money formatting follows DESIGN.md: symbol only in the big figures, currency code in body text, tabular numerals, VND with a reference dollar figure under it.

## Ratings

- Overall design: **7.5 / 10**. The system is a 9; the approval contradiction, the Daily close overflow and the English leakage on the Vietnamese page pull it down because they sit on the three screens that carry the story.
- Against the hackathon's design criterion: **8 / 10**. It looks like a product, not a prototype, and the design supports the claim (check every payment, make the wrong ones impossible to miss). Phuong's blocked screen is a first-class demo moment. DES-1 would be noticed by an attentive judge.
- How design affects the other four criteria:
  - Technological implementation: token discipline, aria on custom controls and keyboard chart show engineering care, while the Daily close overflow and the untranslated errors suggest the demo path was not run end to end at phone width.
  - Potential impact: the Vietnamese onboarding page is the evidence that this serves its market, so an English error on it costs more than its size suggests.
  - Innovation: the check line is the visible form of the idea and it carries the story from the overview to the obligation to the book.
  - Presentation: the first thirty seconds are good on desktop (clear claim, a button that starts the story, a red "Errors prevented $340.00") and weaker on a phone, where the screen is demo chrome (DES-5) and there is a native password prompt before anything (DES-13).

## Am I satisfied?

No. Three Must-fix items are outstanding (DES-1, DES-2, DES-3). DES-2 is a short layout change and DES-3 is mostly string handling; DES-1 needs a small decision on how the reader's output shows on the card. With those closed and DES-4 to DES-7 done I would call it ready for judging.

## What I did not reach

- Tablet widths (768 and 1024: the icon rail), landscape phones.
- States that need data I did not create: payment runs with items, bank file batches, an Autopilot scheduled release with the countdown, a vendor detail with a pending change on a phone, the public remittance and evidence pages (`/remit`, `/evidence`), the new request form with a file uploaded. The Daily close overflow shows these could hide similar problems; DES-2 only appeared after state changed.
- A real screen reader (NVDA, VoiceOver) and speech output; I checked names, roles and landmarks by script.
- Dark mode for every page at 390 (I sampled approval, Daily close, onboarding), and the print stylesheet.
