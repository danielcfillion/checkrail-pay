# Checkrail Pay: practitioner review, round six

Reviewer: AP practitioner for small tour operators in Vietnam, the Philippines and Thailand (PayPal payouts to foreign freelancers, VND/PHP/THB bank transfers, guide cash advances, homestay and driver deposits and balances, group departures, Xero bookkeeper).
Commit tested: 43b40a6. Date: 29 to 30 Oct 2026.

## Bottom line

This is the strongest version I have seen. The controls hold when I push on them: a duplicate, a hand-typed invoice above the contract rate, an unverified new vendor, a bounced payout, a changed PayPal address, a late invoice for a closed month and a removed W-9 were each caught, stopped or re-quoted, and every failure I found fails closed (nothing paid that should not be). The week works end to end for a clerk, an approver, an accountant and an owner, and the Vietnamese remittance messages, the VND bank file, the deposit and balance panel and the advance settlement are things a real operator would use.

What stops me recommending a pilot next month is not the money path but the edges:

1. One Must-fix. A resubmitted invoice (which every vendor does) creates a blocked duplicate that then **blocks the original, legitimate payment** at release. In my run the original failed inside the payment run and could only be freed by the clerk cancelling the duplicate and pressing Check again.
2. Vendor-facing and bookkeeper-facing outputs still contain errors: the run's remittance message names the wrong rail when a vendor gets two payments by different rails, the settlement CSV and the Xero bills file disagree on the advance account code, and the net column means different things on different rails.
3. The real-world gaps are the same as before: no inbound channel (Zalo, email), nothing is sent for the clerk (chase links and remittance texts are copy and paste), the bank file is a generic CSV no bank will accept as is, Wise has never run, and Thailand is only half built.

I am **not** satisfied that no Must-fix item is outstanding: PR6-1 is open.

## New findings

### Must-fix

**PR6-1. A blocked duplicate blocks the original payment at release**

- *What I did.* Lan uploaded Rosa's trip sheet (clean, Ready to pay), then uploaded the same file again, as a vendor chasing "did you get my invoice?" does. The second was correctly Blocked ("already requested for JOB-CEB2D-1024"). Lan built a nine-payment run; Minh released it.
- *What happened.* Rosa's original payment failed in the run: "Execution refused by policy: Reference JOB-CEB2D-1024 was already used by obligation obl_dfd9... (blocked)". Opening the original showed it as Blocked "duplicate reference", pointing at its own duplicate, while the duplicate pointed at the original. The original's page says "Someone with the right role takes this step" to Minh, who cannot cancel (cancel needs the clerk or owner). Recovery worked: Lan cancelled the duplicate, pressed Check again, the original was Ready to pay again and she paid it.
- *Why it matters.* Vendors resend invoices every week. In a pilot this turns the headline control into a source of failed payments in the normal Friday run, and the person who sees the failure (the approver) is not the person who can fix it. It fails closed, so no money is wrong, but a guide or homestay can be paid late because of a duplicate that was correctly stopped.
- *Fix.* The duplicate-reference rule should compare only against requests that are live (approved, in flight, settled) and, among live ones, only against earlier ones. A blocked, rejected or cancelled request must never block the request it duplicates. Also let an approver cancel a request that is blocked as a duplicate, and when a duplicate is created, say on the original's page "a duplicate of this was received and blocked".

### Should-fix

**PR6-2. The invoice's own unit rate is not compared with the contract rate unless the departure is on file**

- *Did.* Typed an invoice "4 days at USD 220 = USD 880" for Phuong Transport (contract USD 200 per vehicle-day) against HG-4D-1004, which had no Phuong line yet.
- *Happened.* One generic warning ("Evidence supplied by operator") and "Awaiting approval". It was blocked only after I imported a manifest that added the line (USD 800), and even then only when someone opened the page or pressed Approve. The Inbox and the run still said "Awaiting approval" and "Has a warning"; the real reason, "USD 80 more than the rate card", appeared only on the payment page.
- *Why.* Many invoices arrive before the departure is keyed in, and the vendor's contract rate is on file and was visible on the invoice.
- *Fix.* When the evidence states a quantity and unit price and the vendor has a rate card for that unit, warn "USD 220 per vehicle-day against contract USD 200" without needing the departure. When a manifest is imported, re-check the open requests for the departures it touched and move any that now break the rate to Blocked.

**PR6-3. The per-vendor remittance message names the wrong rail**

- *Did.* Released a run with two payments each to Maria (JOB-HG4D-0921 by PayPal, ADV-HG4D-1101 by Stellar) and Linh (HS-1027 by Wise, HS-1034 by bank file).
- *Happened.* Maria: "has paid USD 480.00 for JOB-HG4D-0921, ADV-HG4D-1101 by PayPal". Linh: "đang chuyển VND 19,800,000 cho HS-1027, HS-1034 qua chuyển khoản qua Wise" (the doubled "qua" is also clumsy; HS-1034 went by bank file and HS-1027 had already settled).
- *Why.* The message is what the vendor uses to find the money. Telling a homestay owner "Wise" when it is a VCB bulk transfer, or a guide "PayPal" for a Stellar payment, produces a phone call and a loss of trust.
- *Fix.* Group by vendor and rail (one sentence per rail), or name the rail per reference. Use "chuyển khoản ngân hàng" for the bank file and Wise alike, not "qua chuyển khoản qua Wise". Say "đã chuyển" for the settled one and "đang chuyển" for the queued one.

**PR6-4. The settlement CSV and the Xero bills file disagree, and "net" means two things**

- *Did.* Downloaded both files after the run.
- *Happened.* ADV-HG4D-1101 is account 620 in the Xero bills file but "310 Cost of Goods Sold: Contract labour" in the settlement CSV. For PayPal, net is gross plus fee (USD 816.00 for an 800.00 payment); for Stellar and Wise, net equals gross (750.00, VND 10,800,000) although the sender also pays the fee on top (Wise: VND 10,891,090). The settlement description is the raw request text cut mid-word ("...cash advance on advance ADV-HG4D-1101 (bill tota..."). VND amounts print with decimals ("VND 10800000.00") on the Accounting page.
- *Why.* The bookkeeper matches the settlement to the bank feed. The outflow for a Wise payment will not equal the net column, and the advance will be reclassified by hand every month.
- *Fix.* Net = what left the account, on every rail. Use the bill's account code in the settlement file. Use the bill description (vendor, reference, departure), not the request text. Show VND without decimals.

**PR6-5. Aged payables includes unsupported requests and leaves out a queued bank-file payment**

- *Did.* Opened Books after the run.
- *Happened.* The list held two "needs evidence" chat requests (Rosa USD 190, Bayan USD 130) shown by raw id (`obl_e7ee750d9a9748ad`) and Dao's balance; HS-1034 (VND 9,000,000, queued in a downloaded bank file, not yet confirmed) was missing. Total "USD 745 owed".
- *Why.* A chat message with no evidence is not a bill and is not in Xero, so the bookkeeper's payables will not tie to this page. An unconfirmed bank-file payment is still a liability.
- *Fix.* Age approved bills only, show vendor and reference (or the first words of the message, never the id), and keep a payment in ageing until the bank confirms it.

**PR6-6. "Paid to date" on the vendor page counts refunded payments**

- *Did.* Reversed Dao's USD 425 deposit.
- *Happened.* The header stayed at USD 1,615.00 (the old USD 1,190 plus the USD 425 that came back); the fee total also kept the refunded payment's fee.
- *Why.* The owner reads this to judge how much a supplier is worth and to spot a vendor near a threshold.
- *Fix.* Subtract refunded settlements from paid-to-date, and show the refund.

**PR6-7. Bill-linked payments always carry the "typed by operator" warning, which swamps Daily close**

- *Did.* Requested a cash advance, a deposit balance and a pay-again from the vendor page.
- *Happened.* Each has "Evidence supplied by operator, not system; check it against the source documents", even though the system created the evidence record from the bill, and the first Dao payment, made from the original invoice, had none. Daniel's Daily close listed 12 items on a modest day: three "Approved over warnings" that were all this same sentence, plus "New vendor" twice for Linh Family Homestay (two payments, one vendor).
- *Why.* A warning on every payment trains the approver to click through it, and on Autopilot it silently excludes balance payments.
- *Fix.* Mark a bill-created evidence record with its true origin (a bill the business recorded), warn only when a person typed the amount into the evidence, and fold "New vendor" and "Approved over warnings" to one line per vendor and per reason.

**PR6-8. Chasing a vendor and telling a vendor are still copy and paste**

- *Did.* Used "Send them a link to update details" (Dao) and "Ask the vendor for the document" (HS-1027).
- *Happened.* Each makes a one-time link ("Send it over a channel you already use with them"). The vendor page it leads to is in Vietnamese for a Vietnamese vendor, which is good. Nothing is drafted for the clerk to send and nothing is sent; the remittance texts are also copy and paste.
- *Why.* This is the clerk's real Monday: five vendors, five chat windows. It is also the biggest remaining automation gain.
- *Fix.* Draft the message with the link in the vendor's language (the remittance code already does it) with a Copy button and a "sent via Zalo/email/WhatsApp" note; later a Zalo OA or email send. Show a "chased 3 days ago, no reply" list on the vendor page and in Daily close.

**PR6-9. A payout and its reversal in the same statement file are not tied on the first upload**

- *Did.* Uploaded one PayPal CSV holding the payout and its reversal (Reference Txn ID present).
- *Happened.* Both lines were "matched" and the reversal was listed as "could not be tied ... the payout it reverses is only in this same file". Uploading the same file a second time tied it and marked the payment Refunded.
- *Why.* The accountant will think the reversal is unmatched or the app is broken. Unclaimed payouts are returned after 30 days, so the usual case is two files, but any short payout or same-month return hits it.
- *Fix.* Match payouts first, then reversals, inside one pass.

**PR6-10. Thailand is half built, and the Philippine and Vietnamese rails are narrow**

- *Did.* Looked at vendor creation, banks, corridors and withholding.
- *Happened.* Thailand and THB are selectable but there are no Thai banks, no US to Thailand corridor in the enabled list, no Thai withholding (PND 3/53, or the 1%/3% rates). The bank list is Vietnamese only. A Philippine guide is paid by PayPal at about 3% under mid-rate or by PYUSD on Stellar (the router chose Stellar for Maria's USD 300 fuel advance because it delivers more); there is no InstaPay, PESONet, GCash or Maya. Vietnamese personal income tax withholding applies only when the business "pays as a Vietnamese entity", which I did not test.
- *Why.* The target buyer is a regional operator, and a guide cash advance paid in a stablecoin is not something an owner would want as a default.
- *Fix.* Either remove Thailand from the form until it works or add the banks and the withholding. Make "let the router choose" skip crypto rails for advances and for any vendor with no stated preference, and add one local PH rail (a bank file or a PESONet/InstaPay wrapper).

**PR6-11. (carried over) New-vendor callback uses the number typed by the clerk**

- *Fix.* For a vendor with no verified number, require the verifier to enter a second number from an independent source (a booking, the guide licence, an earlier chat) and mark the verification "no independent number".

### Nice-to-have

**PR6-12. Month-end cut-off is silent.** A late invoice for a closed month is posted on the first open day, which is correct, but neither the approver nor the accountant is told that September cost lands in October. Add a "late for a closed month" list to Books for the accrual journal.

**PR6-13. Advance settlement is a typed number.** Lan entered "260" and a note; the app made a USD 40 credit note the vendor owes back and closed the advance. No receipts are attached, no second person looks at the total, and there is no way to collect the USD 40. Accept receipt photos as evidence, have an approver confirm the settled total, and offer to net the credit against the next payment.

**PR6-14. The bank file is not a bank's file.** Columns are generic (`STT, So tai khoan, ...`) and the transfer content is "HS-1034 ad62eb42", with a random suffix the homestay owner will see on their statement. "Returned" needs a recorded reason and I did not complete that step. Map to the VCB and BIDV bulk templates, drop the suffix, and accept the bank's result file to confirm lines automatically.

**PR6-15. Manifest import has no preview.** It reports bad lines by number ("Line 7: no vendor named 'Phuong Transprt'") and imports the rest, and re-importing a departure silently replaces its services. Show a diff first and offer "Add this vendor" for an unknown name.

**PR6-16. The aged payables total mixes currencies without saying so.** One USD figure; VND and PHP are converted at reference rates. Add a per-currency column for the bookkeeper.

## What already works well

- **Controls under pressure.** Each test of mine stopped where it should: duplicate by reference, above-contract invoice, unknown payee from a Vietnamese chat message, an unverified new vendor, changed PayPal address held until a second person called back, a bounced address flagged on re-payment, a removed W-9 re-quoted at 24% backup withholding, a future-dated invoice warned, the departure rate used to block USD 880 against USD 800.
- **Division of work.** Preparer, approver, releaser and accountant are really different people in the app. Releasing a run says plainly how many waiting payments it will approve and re-checks each one just before it goes.
- **Deposit and balance, advance, credit.** One bill, a deposit that is half of it, "Pay the balance" pre-filled, the advance settled against receipts, and the unspent USD 40 as a credit note. This is how tour operators actually work and almost no AP product does it.
- **VND by bank file** with the Napas fee shown, a sender-pays line, and a funding check per rail that says when a balance cannot be read.
- **Vendor-language pages.** Onboarding and remittance pages in Vietnamese for Vietnamese vendors.
- **Month-end handling.** Late invoices are posted on an open day with the original date in the description, not into a closed month.
- **Returned money** reaches the clerk's and owner's lists ("Money came back" first), and Daily close explains each line in one sentence.
- **Honest labelling.** The sidebar and README say plainly what is simulated; I found no screen that claims more than it does.

## Daily tasks that could still be automated

1. Draft and send the chase message and the remittance (PR6-8).
2. Turn a pasted Zalo or email message into a request (the reader works; the channel does not exist).
3. Keep the manifest in step with the booking system (a scheduled pull from Bokun or Rezdy in place of the weekly CSV) and re-check open requests when it changes (PR6-2).
4. Bank statement import to confirm bank-file lines and tie VND returns, as the PayPal statement already does.
5. Receipt photos as evidence for advances, read for totals (PR6-13).
6. A Monday digest for the clerk: chased vendors with no reply, requests waiting for evidence, bills due this week.

## Bottlenecks I hit

- Duplicate resend jams the original (PR6-1).
- Only the approver or owner can import the manifest, and the approver cannot create a request.
- Anything above USD 250 stops for a person, as designed, but every bill-linked payment also carries a warning (PR6-7), so approvers will learn to ignore it.
- The bank file has to be downloaded, hand-uploaded in corporate banking and hand-confirmed.

## What would stop an owner piloting

1. PR6-1 (a resent invoice breaks the original payment).
2. A real inbound channel and a real outbound message for the clerk.
3. A bank file the company's bank accepts, and one live Wise payout to prove the other VND route.
4. Real logins and a proper database, and a backup story.
5. Thailand removed or finished, and a local Philippine rail, if those countries are in the pilot.

## What I did not get to

- Autopilot (schedule, cancel window, pause), and the Daily close sign-off.
- A real vendor PDF invoice (I used the demo PDFs and typed evidence).
- The 1099 list and Form 945 figures, run funding and the English remittance sentence after a tax change, credit-note allocation, and the aged payables in a second currency.
- Bank-file "Returned" to its end, and a Thai or Philippine-registered business setup.
- A Vietnamese-registered business (PIT withholding at 10%) and a Philippine EWT vendor.
- The vendor-side evidence upload page.

## Satisfaction statement

I am not satisfied that no Must-fix item is outstanding. PR6-1 is open: a resent invoice makes the original payment fail at release. The fix is small and I would expect it to be quick. With PR6-1 closed, PR6-3 and PR6-4 corrected and the two warnings in PR6-7 cleaned up, my pilot readiness score would move from 5 to 6, and the pilot scope above would be a reasonable first step.
