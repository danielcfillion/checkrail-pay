**How I tested it.** Same reviewer as 25 Oct. I seeded a fresh database (`data/ind.sqlite`), built, and ran the production server on port 3102 with `CHECKRAIL_USERS="daniel:pw:admin"`, demo personas and a demo VCB source account. I drove it with Playwright as Lan (clerk), Minh (approver), Thu (accountant) and Daniel (owner), and read `books.ts`, `rates.ts`, `users.ts`, `seed.ts`, `xeroBills.ts`, the extractor and the action list. What I did:

- Added a new Vietnamese driver (Hoang Van Duc, VCB account) as Lan, tried to verify him as Lan (refused), verified as Minh by callback.
- Sent his VND invoice before verification (blocked), then after.
- Added a VND rate card, imported a departure manifest CSV (one bad row), and sent an inflated invoice (3 x VND 1,500,000 against a VND 1,200,000 contract rate).
- Used the vendor evidence link to get a revised invoice.
- Approved the Linh VND bill, the Dao deposit, Ana and both Duc invoices, built a six-payment weekly run as Lan and released it as Minh.
- Downloaded and confirmed the VND bank file.
- On the Dao deposit: recorded a USD 85 credit note, applied it, paid the USD 340 balance, sent a remittance advice and opened it as the vendor.
- Gave Maria a USD 300 cash advance and settled it against USD 260 of receipts.
- Removed Jake's W-9 and paid him (24% backup withholding). Turned on VN PIT and paid Duc VND 2,400,000 (10% withheld).
- Tested Minh's USD 1,000 approval limit.
- Wrote a PayPal activity CSV with a manual payment, a missing payout and a payout reversal, and reconciled it as Thu.
- Exported Xero bills and the settlement CSV, closed September, and onboarded a homestay through a one-time link.

The server is stopped (killed by PID). The repo was not changed apart from the new database, the build output and this report. Scripts, test PDFs, CSVs, downloads and screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/11b88efa-03f4-5292-ba70-6074dd94746e/scratchpad/ind2/`.

**Bottom line.** The team built most of the roadmap in a day, and most of it works. A clerk can now onboard a Vietnamese driver, pay him in VND by bank file inside a weekly run, take a deposit and a balance, apply a credit, settle a guide's float, and hand the accountant a Xero file. It has moved from "reviews one payment well" to "a credible AP tool for 10 to 60 suppliers". It is not pilot-ready yet, for three reasons:

1. **Dead ends in the exception flows.** The new features run into each other: a verified vendor stays blocked, a rate-card catch has no short-pay path, and an advance gets blocked by the guide's day rate.
2. **The books disagree with the app.** Credit notes and withholding never reach Xero, invoice dates are not captured, and a returned PayPal payout is missed by reconciliation.
3. **Everything still arrives and leaves by hand.** Nothing comes in by email or Zalo, nobody is notified, and the remittance is copy and paste.

## Findings from hands-on use

**What now works, tested end to end**

- **Vendor master with maker-checker.** Lan added Duc and had no verify control: "You asked for this, so someone else verifies it (daniel, minh can)". Minh verified by callback. The seeded "Tam changed his PayPal" email sits in a side-by-side queue (proposed against on file) with a clear warning. I turned it down.
- **VND end to end.** Linh's VND 10,800,000 bill routed to Wise (mock). Duc's VND bills routed to the bank file ("1.0% more per VND spent than Wise ... settles faster"). The file came out in a Vietnamese bulk format: `STT,So tai khoan,Ten nguoi huong,Ma ngan hang,So tien,Noi dung`.
- **The rate card catches inflation without Claude.** INV-DUC-002 was blocked: "Rate card and departure HG-3D-1023 say VND 3,600,000 (3 vehicle-days x VND 1,200,000); this asks for VND 4,500,000." This was my biggest ask, and it is deterministic and explained.
- **Deposits, credits and balances add up.** The deposit is now USD 425 of USD 850, not 850. The arithmetic was right: credit CN-DML-01 USD 85 applied, balance USD 340, bill closed at "Paid 765, Credited 85, Still open 0".
- **The advance settles properly.** USD 300 advanced against USD 260 of receipts left a USD 40 credit (ADV-MS-01-S) to set against Maria's next invoice. That is the right model.
- **Payment runs.** A six-payment run showed totals per rail, simulated PayPal and Stellar balances, a confirm step, and "each one is checked again just before it goes". The clerk cannot release it. The approver can.
- **Limits and roles hold.** USD 1,260 to Kalaw: "above Minh's approval limit of USD 1,000; daniel can". Thu cannot approve. Lan cannot approve or release.
- **Withholding is right.** Jake without a W-9: "24% backup withholding (USD 108.00) goes to the IRS", and the recipient gets USD 342. Duc under VN PIT: VND 2,400,000 billed, VND 2,160,000 sent. The 1099-NEC list is on Books.
- **Reconciliation catches the two failures that matter most.** It flagged the USD 260 I "paid from phone" to Bayan outside the app as "Money left PayPal with no payment here", and the Kalaw payout missing from the statement.
- **Vendor-facing links work without a login.** Remittance advice in Vietnamese ("Thông báo thanh toán ... Bạn nhận VND 8,333,740"). The single-use evidence link took the revised invoice. The onboarding link refused a second use.
- **Earlier fixes landed.** The period lock works ("closed through 2026-09-30"). Daily close shows local time (GMT+7). The seeded export dates now match the ledger. Settings and README agree on fallback and model. "Errors prevented" is cumulative.

**What breaks, in the order a clerk would hit it**

1. **Verifying a vendor does not unblock their invoice.** INV-DUC-001 was blocked as unverified. After Minh verified Duc it stayed blocked. The only action offered is "Correct to VND 6,000,000", which is disabled because that is already the amount. There is no re-check button. I had to cancel and re-enter the invoice. Every new vendor in week one will hit this.
2. **A rate-card block has no way out except a new invoice.** The block screen offers "Correct to VND 4,500,000", which is the amount that was just blocked. If you correct to the contract amount (3,600,000), it blocks again: "Invoice says VND 4,500,000 (-VND 900,000)". Real AP short-pays to contract and tells the vendor. Here the only route is the evidence link and a revised invoice.
3. **New evidence silently undoes the clerk's correction.** I corrected INV-DUC-002 to VND 3,600,000. When Duc uploaded his revised invoice, the agent re-read the original chat text and set the amount back to 4,500,000. The audit log shows `obligation.amended 360000000`, then `evidence.added`, then `ai.interpreted amountMinor 450000000`. I had to correct it again. The revised invoice also does not supersede the old one, so both stay as evidence ("VND 4,500,000; ... (from the vendor): VND 3,600,000").
4. **A cash advance is blocked by the guide's day rate.** ADV-HG3D-1023, a USD 300 float for fuel and tickets on departure HG-3D-1023, was blocked: "Rate card and departure HG-3D-1023 say USD 135.00 (3 days x USD 45.00)". An advance is not a fee. It went through only after I renamed it so the departure code did not appear. The cancelled attempt left an orphan open advance bill on Maria's page.
5. **The system argues with itself on a balance payment.** The USD 340 balance passed "Settles INV-DML-1010 in full". It also carried two agent warnings: "confidence 70%" and "differs from Invoice by USD 510.00 (lower)". Both are the product's own noise on a payment it built itself. Daily close then lists it under "Approved over warnings ... worth a second look".
6. **The urgency false positive is still there.** The Dao deposit says "50% deposit USD 425 now". It still fires "Urgency language ... social-engineering signal". It is on the main demo inbox.
7. **The bank file is trusted on the clerk's word.** The run page says "paid" for bank-file lines the moment the run is released, before any file reaches a bank. Lan downloaded the file and Lan clicked "The bank paid it", with nothing attached. Two lines to the same payee are not merged. The bank code is "VCB", not a Napas BIN. There is no value date and no debit-account column. The README admits it is a generic CSV.
8. **Reconciliation misses a returned payout.** My CSV had a "Payout Reversal" of +USD 340 from Dao ("Unclaimed payout returned"). The matcher only looks at outgoing lines, so the reversal was ignored, and the app still shows Dao paid in full. Statement-only lines have no action ("record it", "create obligation"). Fees on the statement are not compared to fees in the app.
9. **The Xero bill export is not yet what an accountant can post.**
   - **Invoice date is the day of entry.** INV-DUC-001's PDF says "Date: 22 October 2026"; the export says `2026-10-26`.
   - **Due date only comes from "net N" in the text.** "Net 14" gave `2026-11-08`, 13 days later, because one date is local and the other UTC.
   - **Credit notes are skipped by design** (`b.kind === "credit_note"` is skipped). Xero will show USD 85 open on INV-DML-1010 forever.
   - **Withholding is not on the bill.** The bill is VND 2,400,000 and the payment is 2,160,000, so VND 240,000 hangs open with no PIT payable line. Jake's USD 108 is the same.
   - **The advance is coded `310 Cost of Goods Sold: Contract labour`.** It belongs in an advances or prepayment account. The USD 260 of receipts never become expense lines.
   - **The description is raw chat text.** It even carries the old amount: "...3 ngay xe, 4.500.000 VND" on a 3,600,000 bill.
10. **No due dates means no ageing.** The runs page shows "Due: none" on every payable. Aged payables puts everything in "Not yet due". You cannot build "everything due by Friday".
11. **Routes are fixed when the bill arrives.** Linh's bill was quoted before the bank-file account was configured. After approval it still said "No Vietnamese company bank account is set up" and offered Wise only.
12. **The funding check leaves out fees.** PayPal showed USD 605 needed for 180 + 425. The real debit was about USD 617 with fees. Wise and the bank file show "Balance not available".
13. **The remittance covers only the latest payment.** It does not show the invoice history: 850 billed, 425 deposit, 85 credit, 340 now, zero left. That history is what stops the "you shorted me" message. Each click mints a new link. You copy it into Zalo yourself.
14. **The onboarding page is English-only and asks the wrong things.** It asks a Dong Van homestay for a "Stellar account (public key)". It has no phone number, no tax code (MST) or ID (CCCD), and no W-8BEN upload.
    - **No phone field anywhere in the vendor master.** The main verification method is "Called them back on a number already on file".
    - **No name check.** "VANG THI MAY" as account holder for "Nha nghi Sung La" raised nothing.
    - **"Small test payment" is a dropdown claim.** No test payment is ever sent.
15. **Tax status has no checker.** Lan removed Jake's W-9 status with one Save. She could set it back just as easily, which stops the 24% withholding, with no document and no second person. Payout details have maker-checker; tax status, which also changes how much money leaves, does not.
16. **Daily close is noisy, and one line is wrong.** After one ordinary morning, "Needs a look" had 9 items. Six came from the product's own warnings (urgency, 70% confidence, W-8BEN, "first payment ever"). "First payment ever to Linh Family Homestay" is wrong: Linh has been paid USD 972 before. It is her first bank payment.
17. **"Errors prevented" leaves out rate-card catches.** It stayed at USD 340 after the rate card stopped VND 900,000 on INV-DUC-002. That is the feature most worth bragging about, and the headline number leaves it out.
18. **Timezone leftovers.** The vendor change log says `2026-10-25` for a change made 26 Oct 01:45 GMT+7. "Payout details verified 2026-10-25" sits next to "verified 26 Oct 2026" on the same page. Reconciliation lists Kalaw "on 2026-10-10"; it was 11 Oct local.
19. **Monthly retainers warn every month.** Jake's USD 450 October invoice was held: "Same amount as an earlier payment ... confirm this is a different job". There are no recurring obligations to tell a retainer from a duplicate.
20. **The manifest import is good, but only half the job.** Bad rows are reported by line ("Line 6: no vendor named Unknown Rider Co") and the rest import. But importing a departure does not create expected payables. The clerk still waits for each vendor to invoice.

## 1. Missing tools and processes (what is still lacking)

**Intake (still missing; now the biggest daily cost)**
- Requests still come in by paste or upload only. There is no inbound email address, no Zalo OA, WhatsApp or Messenger webhook, and no ingest API.
- The manifest is a CSV upload. There is no Bokun, Rezdy or Google Sheets connector.

**Outbound communication (partly there)**
- Remittance and evidence links exist but are copy and paste.
- Approvers get no notification, and nobody gets a push, email or Zalo message.
- There is no remittance per run, no reminder on an unanswered evidence link, and no vendor statement of account.

**Invoice data capture (missing)**
- Invoice date, due date, payment terms and the vendor's tax code are not read from the document.
- There is no Vietnamese VAT e-invoice (hóa đơn điện tử) capture or lookup.
- Without these, ageing, "due by Friday" runs and Xero dates are all wrong.

**Exception handling (new gap, created by the new features)**
- Missing: re-check after vendor verification, short-pay to contract with a notice to the vendor, superseding evidence, and line-level expected amounts (rate plus tolls at cost).
- Advances and balances need to skip the fee and amount checks that do not apply to them.

**Accounting completeness (partly there)**
- Xero bills at approval with account code, TaxType and a Departure tracking category: done.
- Still missing:
  - credit notes;
  - withholding liability lines;
  - an advances or prepayment account and receipt lines;
  - attachments on the bill;
  - clean descriptions;
  - FX gain or loss;
  - Xero OAuth (it is still a file import);
  - QuickBooks and MISA.

**Reconciliation (partly there)**
- PayPal CSV matching: done.
- Missing: reversals and returns, a bank statement import (VCB, Techcombank, BIDV), a Wise statement, actions on unmatched lines, and a fee check.

**Bank file controls (partly there)**
- Missing: per-bank templates and Napas BIN codes, one line per payee per run, a value date, a "confirmed by" person different from "downloaded by", and the bank's result file attached as proof.

**Vendor master hardening (partly there)**
- Missing:
  - a phone number;
  - tax ID or CCCD;
  - stored tax forms with expiry, plus licence and insurance expiry;
  - an account-name check (Napas or Wise name lookup);
  - a real penny test;
  - an onboarding page in Vietnamese, Tagalog and English;
  - maker-checker on tax status.

**Recurring payments (missing).** Retainers, vehicle leases, rent and SaaS.

**Reporting (missing)**
- Missing: cost and margin per departure (the tracking data now exists), fees and FX by month, and withholding reports: VN PIT declaration (05/KK-TNCN), a 1099 e-file export, BIR 2307 certificates.

**Platform (missing)**
- SQLite, logins in an environment variable, no 2FA, no backups, one business per database.
- Wise and Xero have not run live. Stellar is still simulated.

## 3. Bottlenecks and fixes

1. **Intake.** Every request is still typed or uploaded by hand. At 250 payables a month that is the clerk's first two hours every day. *Fix:* one forwarding address per business, then a Zalo OA webhook. Attachments go to the PDF reader that already exists.
2. **Dead ends inside the new flows.** Verified-but-blocked, rate-card short-pay, evidence resetting the amount, advances against the rate card. Each one forces cancel and re-enter, which breaks the audit trail and the duplicate history. *Fix:*
   - **Re-check.** Re-run policy on a vendor's blocked items when they are verified, and add a "check again" button.
   - **Short-pay.** Add "Pay contract amount" on an expected-amount block, which records the variance and sends a short-pay notice.
   - **Keep the clerk's amount.** Never re-interpret the amount after a person has amended it.
   - **Supersede.** Let the vendor's upload replace the old evidence.
   - **Exempt.** Advances and system-built balances skip the rate card and the agent's amount guess.
3. **Self-made noise.** Urgency on "now", 70% confidence on balances, "first payment ever" to an old vendor, retainers flagged as duplicates. These train the owner to click past warnings, which is the one habit this product exists to stop. *Fix:* tighten the urgency pattern, suppress agent amount warnings when policy matched a bill, and word the new-bank-details case correctly.
4. **No dates.** Invoice dates and terms are not captured, so runs cannot pick "due by Friday", ageing is empty, and Xero gets today's date. *Fix:* read the date and terms in the extractor (the PDF text already has "Date: 22 October 2026"), let the clerk edit them, and use vendor default terms.
5. **The books drift from the app.** Credit notes and withholding are missing from Xero, the advance is coded to labour, and reversals are not seen. After a month the accountant's Xero aged payables will not match the Books page. *Fix:* export credit notes, put withholding on the bill as a liability line, use an advances account, read reversals on the statement and reopen the payment.
6. **Bank-file trust.** The clerk downloads, uploads and confirms. That is the same person end to end, on their word. *Fix:* the confirmer must differ from the downloader; attach the bank's result file or match the bank statement; per-bank templates.
7. **No one is told anything.** Approvers, vendors and the owner all have to open the app. *Fix:* approval notifications with one-tap approve, automatic remittance per run, evidence-link reminders.
8. **Onboarding friction for rural vendors.** An English form that asks for a Stellar key will be abandoned or filled wrong. *Fix:* a Vietnamese and Tagalog form, bank picked from a list, phone and tax ID fields, and an account-name lookup before the approver's callback.
9. **Platform.** SQLite and environment-variable logins rule out a paying pilot. *Fix:* hosted Postgres with backups, real accounts with 2FA, a business id on every row, and one live run each of Wise and Xero.

## 4. Market and fit

**What changed.** The local bank file and VND invoices mean the product can now serve segment (b), operators based in Vietnam paying domestic suppliers in VND. Yesterday it could not. That is the large segment. The rate card plus manifest check is something none of the obvious substitutes do: Wise Business batch, PayPal mass pay, Xero with Dext, or a Google Sheet. That is the wedge.

**Who would pilot it now**
- **(a) Western outbound operators paying SE Asian suppliers.** They now have Wise or PayPal routing, runs, withholding and a 1099 list. This is the best fit today. Their main gap is Xero OAuth and attachments.
- **(b) Vietnam-based operators.** The bank file and VND are there. They will still miss:
  - Zalo intake and Zalo remittance;
  - MISA export (most Vietnamese SMEs file with MISA, not Xero);
  - VAT e-invoice capture;
  - VietQR or Napas name check;
  - a Vietnamese UI (the app itself is English; only vendor pages are Vietnamese).
- **(c) Bookkeepers with several travel clients.** They want multi-business support, clean Xero (credit notes, withholding, attachments) and statement matching. That is not there yet.

**What would make them switch.** Same as before. Add one more: "the rate card stopped an inflated driver invoice before I paid it". That is now true and demonstrable. Lead with it.

**What competitors will say.** "Wise batch plus a spreadsheet does the payments." True. The answer is control: maker-checker on payout details, a rate card against the manifest, limits per approver, and an audit trail. That is a controller's argument. To win it, the books must match. Today they do not quite.

**Must have before a paid pilot (updated)**
1. Fix the four dead ends and the self-made warnings (finding 1 to 6).
2. Invoice date and terms captured; runs by due date.
3. Xero: credit notes, withholding, attachments, then OAuth. MISA export for Vietnam-based pilots.
4. Inbound email intake, then Zalo.
5. Approval notifications.
6. Bank-file confirm by a second person, with the bank result attached.
7. Hosted Postgres, backups, real logins with 2FA, data-processing terms.
8. One live Wise payout and one live Xero push.

## What it does well

- **Maker-checker on payout details, done properly.** The requester cannot verify, the method is recorded, the old details keep working until a second person checks, and the seeded fraud email shows exactly why. This is the control that saves small operators real money.
- **The rate card plus manifest check.** It is deterministic, explained in one line ("3 vehicle-days x VND 1,200,000"), and needs no model. It is the strongest feature in the product and the right answer to inflated invoices.
- **Bills that understand tour operations.** Deposit, credit note, balance and guide float with a remainder credit. The arithmetic is correct and visible on the vendor page.
- **Payment runs with re-checks.** The clerk prepares, the approver releases, every payment is re-checked at release, and totals are shown per rail.
- **Approval limits and roles that behave.** I could not make any persona do something outside its role.
- **Withholding computed at payment time,** shown on the payment page in plain words.
- **Vendor pages in the vendor's language, without a login.** The remittance in Vietnamese, the single-use evidence and onboarding links.
- **Honesty.** Simulated balances say "simulated". The README lists what is mock, including the generic bank file and the untested Wise adapter. Settings now agrees with the README.

## Ratings

- **Usefulness to the AP clerk: 6/10** (was 4). She can now onboard, pay VND, run a weekly batch, and handle deposits, credits and floats in one place. Intake, chasing and vendor messages are still manual, and four exception dead ends will push her back to "cancel and re-enter" several times a week.
- **Usefulness to the owner: 7/10** (was 6). Limits, cover while away, runs with a funding view, maker-checker on bank changes and a local-time Daily close are what an owner delegates on. That is held back by no notifications, a close page padded with the product's own warnings, and no cost-per-departure view.
- **Completeness as an AP product: 6/10** (was 3). Vendor master, multi-currency, partials, runs, rate cards, reconciliation, ageing, period lock and withholding all exist now. Intake, notifications, recurring bills, bank statements and an accounting export that matches the app are the gaps.
- **Readiness for a real pilot: 4/10** (was 2). A friendly operator could run it in shadow mode next to their current process. For real money it still needs Postgres and real logins, a live Wise or bank-template run, Xero that agrees with the Books page, and the dead ends fixed.
- **Strength as a hackathon entry: 8/10** (unchanged). The breadth now answers a judge with SE Asia experience: VND bills, a Vietnamese bank file and a rate card that catches an inflated driver invoice. Against that, the PayPal story is still two sandbox payouts, Stellar is simulated, and a false "urgency" warning sits on the main demo screen. Lead the video with the rate-card catch and maker-checker, not the routing.

**Worth saving to memory:** the 26 Oct re-audit rated Checkrail Pay clerk 6, owner 7, completeness 6, pilot readiness 4, hackathon 8. The top fixes are four exception dead ends:

1. A blocked invoice is not re-checked after vendor verification.
2. A rate-card block has no short-pay path.
3. A vendor evidence upload resets an amended amount.
4. A cash advance is blocked by the guide's rate card.

Next come warning noise (urgency on "now", balance warnings), invoice date and terms capture, and Xero completeness (credit notes, withholding, attachments). PayPal reconciliation misses payout reversals.
