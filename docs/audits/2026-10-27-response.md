# Response to the round-three audits of 27 Oct 2026

Both reports are in this folder (`security-2026-10-27.md`, `industry-2026-10-27.md`).

## Security

| Finding | Status | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| H1 Login attribution inert at runtime | Fixed, checked over HTTP | Every server action now runs inside `runAs(login, ...)` (AsyncLocalStorage.run), and the login comes from the persona header when a persona is active (the actor header names the persona). The first version set the login with enterWith inside an awaited helper, which never reached the caller. Pages run their people checks the same way. Reproduced the round-three attack on a built server: owner approves USD 6,000, switches to the Daniel persona, approves again: refused, one approval recorded with login `owner`. A test covers the login surviving awaits and helpers. |
| H2 Reversal refunds with no binding | Fixed | A reversal is tied to a payment only through PayPal's Reference Txn ID, and only to a payout line this business already matched to its own settlement; amount, currency and sender must also agree; one reversal refunds at most one payment. Lines that cannot be tied are listed for a person, with the reason. Only someone who reconciles (accountant, approver, owner) can apply a refund; a clerk's upload lists it. |
| M1 Clerk changes the vendor's country | Fixed | Country joins tax type, tax form, withholding, phone and retainer behind `verify_vendor`. |
| M2 Dual-approval deadlock | Fixed | Above the dual amount the second signature is a check, not the authority: once someone whose limit covers the amount has approved, any other approver may give the second approval. Two different people still sign. The warning now fires only when nobody can approve the amount or fewer than two people can approve at all. |
| L1 Vendor evidence supersedes business records | Fixed | A record that came in through an integration is replaced only by a revision with the same invoice number. Business-made kinds (job sheet, booking, purchase order) are never replaced. |
| L2 Amend accepts vendorId | By design | Only for a request with no matched payee; the person becomes a preparer and someone else approves. |
| L3 Period lock local-day bucketing | Accepted | As before. |
| L4 Bank-file downloader query not business-scoped | Accepted for now | It filters by batch id, which is random and unique; scope it with multi-business support. |
| I1 CSP allows inline script | Accepted | Next.js needs it today; no sink found. |

## Industry

| Finding | Status | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real PayPal reversal missed | Fixed | Incoming lines are checked against the sender (From), and tied by Reference Txn ID. |
| 2 Caught reversal drops the vendor out of every list | Fixed | The bill reopens when money is owed again; a refunded payment shows "Pay it again", which goes through every check; it counts in aged payables until requested again; Daily close shows "Money came back" first. |
| 3 Date edits walk through the period lock | Fixed | The old and new invoice dates must both be in an open period; a future invoice date is refused; a change to a bill already in Xero is listed on the Accounting page to repeat in Xero. |
| 4 Tax change after intake leaves the quote stale | Fixed, and a real bug behind it | Execution now sends the amount as policy just recomputed it (it used the obligation as loaded before policy ran, so tax added after approval was not kept back). Any difference between the quote and what will be sent re-quotes, so the settlement and remittance record what the vendor got. |
| 5 Remittance says transferred while the bank file is queued; one message per payment; English rail name | Fixed | "On its way" (đang chuyển) until confirmed; one message per vendor per run; bank rails named in Vietnamese. |
| 6 Advance remainder coded to labour | Fixed | The settlement credit of an advance goes to the advances account (620). Credit-note allocation in Xero is still by hand. |
| 7 Only the owner can record tax forms | Fixed | Approvers open the vendor's edit page for tax, country, phone and retainer (no payout form). Tax forms as documents with expiry: not done. |
| 8 Callback number for a new vendor comes from the same request | Open | Documented; the missing-phone warning stays. |
| 9 Daily close padded | Fixed | Missing tax form and first payment are one line per vendor; returned money ranks first. |
| 10 Small things | Partly | "Apply a credit" pre-fills the credit left. Bank reference matching, net-of-tax ageing and phone formatting: not done. |

## Round four (verification), 27 Oct
