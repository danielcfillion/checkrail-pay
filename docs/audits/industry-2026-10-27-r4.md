# Checkrail Pay: practitioner verification pass, round four

**How I tested it.** Same reviewer as the 25, 26 and 27 Oct reports. I used a fresh copy of HEAD daf268c. I seeded `data/ind4.sqlite`, built, and ran the production server on port 3106 with demo personas and the VCB source account. I drove it with Playwright as Lan, Minh, Thu and Daniel, then stopped it by PID (27899, 27898, 27854). I read the team's response (`docs/audits/2026-10-27-response.md`) and then replayed the case behind each of my 27 Oct findings 1 to 10. I also tried a few cases next to them. Scripts, PDFs and CSVs are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/11b88efa-03f4-5292-ba70-6074dd94746e/scratchpad/ind2/r4/`. The repo was not changed apart from the database, the build output and this report.

**Bottom line.** All the money-path findings from 27 Oct are fixed:

- **Reversals.** A PayPal reversal in the real format is now tied by its Reference Txn ID. Only someone who reconciles can apply it, the bill reopens, and the vendor shows up in ageing and Daily close.
- **Dates.** Date edits respect the period lock and refuse future dates, and an edit to a bill already in Xero is listed for repeating there.
- **Tax after intake.** Removing a W-9 now changes what is sent, what is recorded and what the remittance says, by either payment path.
- **Remittances.** They say "on its way" and come one per vendor.

The re-payment flow added two new bookkeeping errors:

1. "Pay it again" exports a **second Xero bill with the same invoice number**.
2. The **1099 list counts refunded payments**.

There are also three smaller gaps: a reversal with no Reference Txn ID has nowhere to go, re-paying an address that just bounced raises no warning, and a late invoice dated in a closed month goes straight into Xero dated in that month.

## New findings

1. **"Pay it again" exports a duplicate Xero bill.** After Jake's INV-JM-0095 payout was reversed and paid again, Accounting offered "Xero bills to import (2)". The file holds INV-JM-0095 again: 200.00 to 400, and -48.00 to 825. The original bill was exported on approval. Importing this file gives Xero two bills for one invoice, so payables are overstated by USD 152 until someone deletes one. The returned money is also not in the accounting export at all. The settlement CSV still shows INV-JM-0095 paid once, with no reversing entry. The bookkeeper finds the reversal only in the bank feed. *Fix:* when paying again, reuse the existing bill and do not export it twice. Export the reversal as a receipt or a negative payment against the bill.
2. **The 1099 list counts refunded payments.** After the reversal, Books shows Jake "Paid this year USD 1,550.00, Withheld USD 156.00". The 200 that came back is still counted, along with its USD 48 withholding. When the re-payment settles, both are counted twice. `form1099Report` sums every settlement without looking at the payment's state. For a vendor near the USD 2,000 threshold this decides whether a 1099 is filed. The withholding figure feeds Form 945. *Fix:* leave out settlements whose payment is refunded.
3. **A reversal with no Reference Txn ID has nowhere to go.** Maria's +180 reversal is listed as "Look these up in PayPal". That is right for a line the app cannot prove. But there is no action to tie it by hand once the accountant has checked, and it does not appear in Daily close. In the app Maria stays paid while her money sits back in the business's PayPal balance. Actions on statement-only lines are a known gap; this case makes it a money gap, not just tidiness.
4. **Re-paying an address that just bounced is not flagged.** The Dao balance came back as an unclaimed payout. The new balance request passed 10 of 10 rules to the same `dao.lodge@example.com`, with "details verified 14 Aug 2026". The page tells the clerk to check the details first, but policy does not. It was held only because USD 340 is over the USD 250 line. Under USD 250 on Assist, or within caps on Autopilot, it could go straight back to the same dead address. *Fix:* a warning that holds any payment to payout details whose last payment came back, until someone re-verifies them.
5. **A late invoice dated in a closed month goes into Xero in that month.** INV-DUC-005, dated 20 September, came in on 26 Oct with September closed. It was taken in, approved and exported as `2026-09-20`, with no warning. Edits are refused for that date, but intake is not checked. A Xero lock date would refuse or misplace it. Ageing correctly shows it "29 days late". *Fix:* at intake, keep the document date but post the bill on the first open day, or hold it for the accountant. A future-dated invoice at intake (INV-DUC-004, dated 30 Oct) is also accepted silently; only the edit path refuses future dates.
6. **"Pay it again" downgrades the evidence.** The new INV-JM-0095 payment shows the original PDF evidence as "typed by operator" and fails "Evidence supplied by operator, not system; check it against the source documents". The approver now sees a warning about a document that was machine-read the first time. *Fix:* carry the original evidence over with its provenance.
7. **Leftovers on tax after intake.**
   - **The route panel still quotes the gross before payment.** On a ready-to-pay payment it says "$200.00 delivered to the recipient, Sender pays USD 200.25", though USD 48 will be held back. The recorded figures are right after payment.
   - **Run funding uses the gross too.** "USD 805.00 to payees" includes Jake at 200 rather than 152. That is safe as a funding total, but it is not what leaves PayPal.
   - **The English remittance message omits the withholding.** It says "has paid USD 200.00 for INV-JM-0095". The page behind the link shows the withholding correctly.
8. **Two instructions for the same refund.** Daily close and the reconciliation result say "pay it again from the payment page". For a bill-linked payment, the payment page says "pay what is open on it from the vendor's page". Both work, but the clerk is sent to the wrong page first.
9. **Overlapping statements list already-reconciled payments.** Uploading a second, partial statement for the same dates listed four payments, already matched by the first file, as "Paid here, not on the statement". Real monthly files will not overlap, so this is low priority. Payments already reconciled against an earlier statement should be skipped.

**No regressions found.** I smoke-tested the main pages (overview, inbox, vendors, runs, departures, books, bank files, accounting, close, settings, audit, ledger, exceptions) and every 26 and 27 Oct fix I replayed. All loaded and behaved as in round three. The server log was clean.

## Ratings

- **Usefulness to the AP clerk: 7/10** (unchanged). Returned money now comes back into her queue with a clear next step, and remittances are one per vendor with honest wording. Intake, sending messages and notifications are still manual. Those, not the money path, now cap her score.
- **Usefulness to the owner: 8/10** (unchanged). Daily close is tighter and ranks returned money first. The approver can carry tax forms, so the owner is no longer the bottleneck. The 1099 overstatement is the kind of number an owner signs without checking, so it needs fixing before year end.
- **Completeness as an AP product: 7/10** (unchanged). The reversal and period-lock holes are closed. Duplicate bills on re-payment, no reversal entry in the books, and untied reversals with no action are new gaps of the same size. Intake, notifications, bank statements and reporting are still not built.
- **Readiness for a real pilot: 5/10** (unchanged). The three money-path fixes I asked for on 27 Oct are in and work. Postgres, real logins, one live Wise payout and Xero OAuth are still missing, and the two new bookkeeping bugs would surface in the pilot's first month-end.
- **Strength as a hackathon entry: 8/10** (unchanged). The demo is clean and the controls story is now consistent end to end. A live PayPal Transaction Search reconciliation that ties a real reversal by its Reference Txn ID would be the strongest PayPal moment the entry could show, and would move it to 9.

**Worth saving to memory:** the 27 Oct round-four verification of Checkrail Pay (HEAD daf268c) found all money-path findings from round three fixed: reversals tied by Reference Txn ID, dates inside the period lock, re-quote after a tax change, and one remittance per vendor. The new issues are:

- The 1099 report counts refunded payments.
