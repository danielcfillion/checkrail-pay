# Checkrail Pay: practitioner audit, round three (AP operations, SE Asia tour operators)

**How I tested it.** Same reviewer as 25 and 26 Oct. I used a fresh copy of HEAD 2c76c81. I seeded `data/ind3.sqlite`, built, and ran the production server on port 3103 with demo personas and the source account "VCB 0071009999999 Ridgeline Vietnam". I drove it with Playwright as Lan (clerk), Minh (approver), Thu (accountant) and Daniel (owner), and read the code where a result needed explaining (`books.ts`, `bankFiles.ts`, `vendors.ts`, `orchestrator.ts`, `InvoiceDates.tsx`). I replayed the 26 Oct script and added the cases the team said were fixed:

- **New vendor.** Duc added with phone, tax ID and 7-day terms. His invoice was blocked while unverified, then Minh verified him.
- **Inflated invoice.** Rate card plus manifest, then a short pay to the rate card, then a revised invoice through the evidence link.
- **Weekly run.** Five approvals, then a run picked "due by 2 Nov". Linh's VND bill was re-quoted and joined the bank file.
- **Bank file.** Downloaded by Lan, Lan's confirm refused, confirmed by Minh with a bank reference.
- **Remittances.** Messages for the whole run.
- **Deposit and balance.** Dao deposit, credit note, balance, and the remittance with invoice history.
- **Advances.** One against a departure. One cancelled. A settlement against receipts.
- **Tax.** Lan tried to remove Jake's W-9 (refused). The owner removed it, then Jake was paid. VN PIT applied to Duc.
- **PayPal reconciliation.** A statement with a manual payment, a missing payout, a fee difference and a payout reversal.
- **Xero export.** Bills exported to Xero.
- **Period close and dates.** September closed, then I tried to backdate an invoice into it.
- **Onboarding.** A homestay with a mismatched account name and no phone.

The server is stopped (killed by PID 13865, 13864, 13847). The repo was not changed apart from the database, the build output and this report. Scripts, PDFs, CSVs and downloads are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/11b88efa-03f4-5292-ba70-6074dd94746e/scratchpad/ind2/r3/`.

**Bottom line.** The team fixed almost everything I called a dead end on 26 Oct, and the fixes hold up in use:

- A verified vendor's invoice unblocks itself.
- A rate-card catch can be short-paid with a note to the vendor.
- A clerk's correction survives a revised invoice, which replaces the old one.
- Advances no longer trip the rate card.
- The deposit and balance flow is clean.
- Invoice dates and terms come off the PDF.
- Runs pick by due date.
- The bank file merges per payee and needs a second person to confirm.

Three new problems came out of the new features, plus one leftover:

1. **A real PayPal reversal is still missed,** and when one is caught the vendor drops out of every list.
2. **Editing an invoice date walks straight through the period lock** and leaves Xero out of date.
3. **A tax change after intake leaves the quote stale,** so the vendor's remittance says they received money that went to the IRS.
4. **Intake and notifications are still fully manual.** This is known and not built.

This is now a tool I would put in front of a friendly operator in shadow mode. It is not yet one I would let move their money unsupervised.

## Findings from hands-on use

**Verified fixed (the team's list, checked one by one)**

- **Re-check after verification.** INV-DUC-001 was blocked as unverified. When Minh verified Duc, it moved itself to "Awaiting approval". Blocked items also offer "Fixed the cause somewhere else? Check again".
- **Short pay to the rate card.** The block now says "The rate card says VND 3,600,000. Paying that is a short pay" and offers "Correct to VND 3,600,000". After correcting, it passed to approval with a clear warning: "Short pay to the rate card ... Tell the vendor why VND 900,000 is not paid."
- **The amendment survives evidence, and revised invoices replace old ones.** After Duc uploaded his revised invoice, the amount stayed VND 3,600,000. "Evidence says" now shows only "Invoice INV-DUC-002 (from the vendor): VND 3,600,000".
- **Advances.** ADV-HG3D-1023 (USD 300, same departure as Maria's day rate) passed 10 of 10 rules. A cancelled advance (ADV-MS-02) shows "void" on the vendor page. The receipt settlement left a USD 40 credit as before.
- **Bill-built payments are clean.** The USD 340 Dao balance passed 10 of 10 rules, with no confidence or amount warnings.
- **Deposit "now" is no longer urgency.** The Dao deposit shows only "Amount exceeds auto-execute threshold".
- **Dates and terms.** INV-DUC-001 read "Invoice date 22 Oct 2026, Due 29 Oct 2026" from the PDF and Duc's 7-day terms. Jake's "net 14" from 20 Oct gave 3 Nov, which is correct now. The runs page shows due dates. "Pick everything due by 2026-11-02" selected exactly the four that were due.
- **Xero export.**
  - Invoice dates come from the documents.
  - Withholding is a negative line on account 825 ("Tax withheld: No W-9 on file, -108.00"; VN PIT -240,000).
  - Credit notes are exported as negative bills.
  - Advances go to 620.
  - Descriptions are clean ("Transport services INV-DUC-002, departure HG-3D-1023").
  - The export is now incremental ("No new bills for Xero").
- **Fee differences.** "Phuong Transport statement USD 14.50, recorded USD 12.00".
- **Bank file.**
  - Bank-file lines show "queued for the bank" after release.
  - Lan downloaded the file. When she tried to confirm, the button did nothing. Minh confirmed with reference FT26299123456, and the page shows "Confirmed by minh ..., bank reference FT26299123456".
  - Duc's two invoices became one line of VND 9,600,000 with both references in the content.
  - The file now has `Ngay gia tri` (value date) and `Tai khoan nguon` (source account) columns.
- **Funding includes fees.** "PayPal USD 183.60: USD 180.00 to payees + USD 3.60 fees".
- **The remittance carries the invoice history.** Dao's page lists: total USD 850, deposit 425, balance 340 "(lần này)" (this time), credit CN-DML-01 -85, remaining 0. The text message ends "Còn lại: USD 0.00" (still owed: USD 0.00).
- **Onboarding.**
  - The form has a bank picker (VCB, BIDV, CTG, AGR, TCB and others), a phone field, a tax code (MST) or ID (CCCD) field, and a "Tiếng Việt" switch. The Stellar field is gone.
  - The verifier sees: "The name on the account (VANG THI MAY) does not match Nha nghi Sung La. Often it is the owner's own account; ask whose it is when you call." and "No phone number given."
- **Wording.** "First payment to Linh Family Homestay's bank account (earlier ones went by PayPal)."
- **Errors prevented** went from USD 340.00 to USD 375.44 after the rate-card catch (VND 900,000).
- **Quote again** re-routed Linh's VND bill to the bank file once the source account was set.

**What still breaks or misleads, in order of money at risk**

1. **A real PayPal reversal is not recognised.**
   - I used the line as PayPal's activity download writes it: "Payout Reversal", +340.00, From `dao.lodge@example.com`, To `ap@ridgeline.example`.
   - It appeared under "Money left PayPal with no payment here" as "USD -340.00 to Dao Mountain Lodge".
   - The matcher compares the line's *To* address with the vendor's email. On an incoming line the To address is the business itself.
   - It only worked when I blanked the To column.
2. **When a reversal is recognised, the vendor drops out of every list.** After the blank-To test:
   - The balance payment showed "Refunded. No actions available in this state."
   - The bill said "Invoice INV-DML-1010 · closed" right above "USD 340.00 open of USD 850.00", with no "Pay the balance" button.
   - Aged payables dropped to USD 94.49 and did not list Dao.
   - Daily close did not mention it at all.
   - The reconciliation text says "check their details, then request it again", but there is no way to request it again. Dao is owed USD 340 and nothing in the app shows it.
3. **Editing an invoice date walks through the period lock.** With books closed through 30 Sept, Lan changed INV-DUC-003's invoice date from 28 Oct to 15 Sept and got "Dates saved." `setObligationDates` has no `assertOpenPeriod` check. That bill had already gone to Xero dated 28 Oct, and Accounting now says "No new bills for Xero", so the app and Xero disagree with no flag. The same control also accepted a future-dated invoice (28 Oct, entered on 26 Oct) without comment.
4. **A tax change after intake leaves the quote stale, and the remittance misstates what was paid.**
   - Jake's invoice was taken in with a W-9 on file. The owner then removed the W-9. Approval correctly applied 24% (USD 108 withheld, USD 342 paid).
   - But the route still said "recipient gets USD 450.00", and the settlement recorded "Paid USD 342.00 ... Received USD 450.00".
   - Jake's remittance page reads "Amount USD 450.00 / You receive USD 450.00 / Tax withheld USD 108.00".
   - The refresh-before-pay only runs when the quote is over 6 hours old, and only when the router picks the rail. "Pay with PayPal" skips it.
5. **The run remittance says "transferred" before the bank has paid.** The run's Vietnamese messages for Duc say "đã chuyển VND 6,000,000 ... qua Bank file" (has transferred). They were generated straight after release, while the file was still "queued for the bank". The linked page correctly says "Đang chuyển" (transferring).
   - Duc gets two messages for what the bank file merged into one transfer.
   - "Bank file" is left in English inside the Vietnamese sentence.
   - The short-pay note in the remittance was not exercised: the revised invoice turned it into a full payment.
6. **The advance remainder is coded to labour.** The settlement credit ADV-HG3D-1023-S (-USD 40) went to account 310 Contract labour. The advance went to 620. So 620 keeps USD 300 forever and labour is understated by 40. Receipts as expense lines are a known gap. The credit should still go back to 620. Credit notes are exported but not allocated to the invoice they reduce, so the accountant matches CN-DML-01 to INV-DML-1010 by hand in Xero.
7. **Only the owner can record a tax form.** Lan is now refused, which is right. But Minh, the approver, is redirected away from the vendor edit page, so in practice only Daniel can record a W-9, W-8BEN or retainer. With 30 to 60 foreign vendors, every W-8BEN waits on the owner. Tax forms are still a dropdown with no document attached.
8. **The callback number for a new vendor comes from the same request.** Lan typed Duc's phone when she created him. Minh's verification is recorded as "called them back on a number already on file". The clerk can set the phone the first time; only changes need an approver. That is reasonable, but for a brand-new vendor "already on file" means "from the same request". The missing-phone warning only fires when the phone is blank.
9. **Daily close is still padded.** After a normal morning it had 8 items:
   - three "Approved over warnings", two of them only for a missing W-8BEN, which repeats on every payment to the same vendor;
   - "First payment ever to Hoang Van Duc" twice, for one merged transfer.
   - The one item that needed a look, the USD 340 that came back, was not there.
10. **Small things.**
    - "Apply a credit" still pre-fills the invoice's open amount (USD 425) rather than the credit left (USD 85).
    - The bank reference accepts any 4 to 64 characters and is never checked against anything.
    - Aged payables shows INV-DUC-003 at the gross VND 2,400,000, though VND 240,000 of it is owed to the tax office.
    - Phone numbers display unformatted ("+84912000111").

## 1. Missing tools and processes (what is still lacking)

**Intake (known, not built; now the largest daily cost)**
- Requests still come in by paste or upload. There is no forwarding address, no Zalo OA, WhatsApp or Messenger webhook, and no ingest API.
- The manifest is a CSV upload with no Bokun, Rezdy or Google Sheets sync.

**Notifications (known, not built)**
- Approvers are not told anything is waiting.
- Remittance and evidence links are still copied into Zalo by hand.
- There are no reminders on unanswered evidence links.

**Returned and reversed payments (new gap)**
- Recognise reversals on PayPal's real line format.
- Reopen the bill and offer "pay again" after the vendor's details are re-checked.
- Show it on Daily close and in ageing.

**Period integrity (new gap)**
- Date edits must respect the lock and warn on future dates.
- A changed bill already sent to Xero must be flagged for re-export or a Xero edit.

**Statement reconciliation (partly)**
- PayPal CSV matching and the fee check work.
- Missing: bank and Wise statement import, actions on statement-only lines, and a check of the bank reference against the statement.

**Advance accounting (partly)**
- Receipts are typed as a total, not uploaded and coded (known).
- The remainder credit is coded to labour.

**Bank file (partly)**
- Merged per payee, value date and source account: done.
- Per-bank templates and Napas BIN codes are not done (known). The source account column carries a label, not just an account number. The value date is ISO, where VN bank portals usually want dd/mm/yyyy.

**Vendor master (mostly)**
- Phone, tax ID, terms, retainer, name and phone warnings: done.
- Missing: tax forms as documents with expiry, an approver able to record them, licence and insurance expiry, an account-name lookup (Napas or Wise), and a real penny test.

**Expected payables from the manifest (known, not built).**

**Reporting (known, not built).** Departure P&L, fees and FX by month, VN PIT 05/KK-TNCN, a 1099 e-file export, BIR 2307.

**Platform (known, not built).** SQLite, logins in an environment variable, no 2FA or backups, one business per database. Wise and Xero have not run live, Xero is still a file import with no attachments, and Stellar is simulated.

## 3. Bottlenecks and fixes

1. **Intake.** Still the first two hours of the clerk's day. *Fix:* a forwarding address per business that feeds the existing PDF reader, then Zalo OA.
2. **Returned money is invisible.** A reversal is missed on PayPal's real format; when caught, the vendor vanishes from ageing and close. *Fix:*
   - Match incoming lines on the From address.
   - Set the bill back to open.
   - Show "pay again", which goes through re-verification if the payout was unclaimed.
   - Put returned money at the top of Daily close.
3. **Dates versus the books.** A clerk can move a bill into a closed month. *Fix:*
   - Apply `assertOpenPeriod` to both the old and the new date.
   - Refuse future invoice dates, or warn on them.
   - Mark the bill "changed since export" so the accountant re-sends or edits it in Xero.
4. **Stale quotes.** Any change to what is payable (withholding, short pay, currency) must re-quote before money moves and before a remittance is written. *Fix:* always re-quote at execute when the payable amount differs from the quote's. Write "received" from the rail's answer, never from the quote.
5. **Owner bottleneck on tax forms.** *Fix:* let approvers record tax status and retainers. Attach the form, with an expiry for W-8BEN (three years).
6. **Close noise.** *Fix:* raise "no W-8BEN" once per vendor, not per payment. Group "first payment" per vendor per day. Rank returned money and refunds above everything else.
7. **Bank-file handling at the portal.** The clerk still logs in to VCB, uploads, waits and reads the reference. *Fix:* per-bank templates now; a bank statement import next, so the reference and amount are matched, not typed.
8. **No one is told anything.** *Fix:* approval notifications with a one-tap link, and remittance messages sent on the vendor's channel after the bank confirms.
9. **Platform.** *Fix:* Postgres, real accounts with 2FA, backups, one live Wise payout and one Xero OAuth push.

## 4. Market and fit

**What changed since 26 Oct.** The two features that make this product different from "Wise batch plus a spreadsheet" are the rate card against the manifest and maker-checker on payout details. Both now hold up through the messy cases: short pay, revised invoices, advances, a second person on the bank file. Errors prevented now counts rate-card catches, so the owner's headline number tells the story the product is built on.

**Who would pilot it now**
- **(a) Western outbound operators paying SE Asian suppliers.** This is the best fit. They need Xero OAuth with attachments and a live Wise run. The rest is there.
- **(b) Vietnam-based operators paying in VND.** This is now plausible: VND bills, a merged bank file, Vietnamese vendor pages, VN PIT. They will still want MISA, VAT e-invoice capture, Zalo in and out, a Vietnamese back office, and the per-bank templates.
- **(c) Bookkeepers with several clients.** They need multi-business support and an export that never drifts from the app. The date-edit hole and the reversal gap are exactly what they would find first.

**What would make them switch.** The rate-card catch with a short-pay note to the vendor, shown on one screen. Maker-checker on bank changes, with the name-mismatch warning. A remittance in Vietnamese that shows deposit, credit and balance. Lead the pitch with these, in that order.

**Must have before a paid pilot (updated)**
1. A returned or reversed payout recognised on PayPal's real format, with the bill reopened and the vendor visible in ageing and close.
2. Date edits inside the period lock, with re-export flagged.
3. A re-quote whenever the payable amount changes; remittances written from the rail's answer.
4. Inbound email intake.
5. Approval notifications.
6. Approvers able to record tax forms, with the document attached.
7. Hosted Postgres, backups, real logins with 2FA.
8. One live Wise payout and one Xero OAuth push with an attachment.

## What it does well

- **Exception handling is now part of the design.** Check again, short pay, superseding evidence, a void on cancel. Each one is a single button with a sentence saying why. This was the weakest area on 26 Oct and is now a strength.
- **The rate card plus manifest.** Deterministic, explained, with a short-pay path that tells the clerk what to say to the vendor. Still the best feature in the product.
- **Payment runs a controller would sign off.**
  - Due-by selection, and fees in the funding total.
  - A re-check at release.
  - Bank-file lines held as "queued for the bank" until a second person enters the bank's reference.
- **Maker-checker extends to the right fields.** Payout details, phone changes, tax status and retainers are refused to the clerk. The verifier sees name-mismatch and missing-phone warnings in plain words.
- **The Xero export a bookkeeper can mostly post.** Document dates, terms, departure tracking, withholding to 825, credit notes, advances to 620, clean descriptions, incremental export.
- **Vendor-facing pages.** A Vietnamese remittance with the whole invoice history and the tax withheld. A single-use evidence link whose upload replaces the old invoice. An onboarding form with a bank picker.
- **Honesty.** Simulated balances are labelled. The README says what is mock, including the generic bank file and the untested Wise adapter. The team's list of "known, not done" matched what I found.

## Ratings

- **Usefulness to the AP clerk: 7/10** (was 6, then 4). The exception flows now work, so she no longer cancels and re-enters. Dates, terms and due-by runs remove a daily chore. Intake and messaging are still manual, and a reversed payout leaves her no way to pay the vendor again.
- **Usefulness to the owner: 8/10** (was 7, then 6). Errors prevented now counts the rate-card catches. Runs show the real funding need. The bank file cannot be confirmed by the person who uploaded it. Maker-checker covers tax and phone. It is held back by no notifications, being the only person who can record tax forms, and a Daily close that still misses returned money.
- **Completeness as an AP product: 7/10** (was 6, then 3). Every core AP object now exists and mostly agrees with the books. The gaps are intake, notifications, bank statements, recurring bills, departure reporting, and the reversal and period-lock holes.
- **Readiness for a real pilot: 5/10** (was 4, then 2). I would run it in shadow mode beside a real operator's process tomorrow. Before it moves real money, it needs the three money-path fixes (reversals, dates in the lock, re-quote on payable change), Postgres and real logins, and one live Wise and Xero run.
- **Strength as a hackathon entry: 8/10** (unchanged). The demo is now clean: no false urgency, and a deposit, short pay and bank file that a judge with SE Asia experience will respect. The PayPal integration is still two sandbox payouts plus read-only tools, and Stellar is simulated. One live PayPal reconciliation pulled through Transaction Search, the scope they already have, would move it to 9.

**Worth saving to memory:** the 27 Oct round-three audit rated Checkrail Pay clerk 7, owner 8, completeness 7, pilot readiness 5, hackathon 8. All 26 Oct dead ends are fixed. The new money-path issues are:

1. PayPal reversals are missed on the real line format (To = business), and a caught reversal closes the bill with no pay-again path and drops out of ageing and Daily close.
2. Invoice-date edits bypass the period lock and are not re-exported to Xero.
3. A withholding change after intake leaves the quote stale, so the remittance says "You receive USD 450" when 342 was paid.
