# Checkrail Pay design review, round 8 (track C, cycle 3 sign-off)

Reviewer: the same independent design reviewer as rounds 6 and 7. Checkout `review-c3` at commit f6e6c1e (combined design, security and business fixes), compared with 54e17d0. Design only.

## Bottom line

I can sign off. The Must-fix from cycle 2 (NEW-1) is closed on every state I could reach, the "waiting on you" counts now agree everywhere for all four roles, the Daily close tab order is its reading order, and a 148-measurement overflow sweep found nothing. The business and security changes did not damage the design: Books, Accounting, vendor pages, the run page, remittance and the new Waiting on someone else panel all look finished and hold up at 390 px in both themes.

What is left is a short Should-fix list, and the top of it is the same theme as NEW-1 in a quieter form: three places still show a reader concern less loudly than the approval page does (the Ready to pay band's green tone, the overview autopilot countdown, and the run page lines). None makes a false statement, which is why I do not call them Must-fix.

- Fresh database in `review-c3/data/`, production build, `next start` on port 3137, demo personas on, one admin login, mock rails. Server pid 10954, stopped by that pid at the end; the checkout is clean.

I could not reach drafted, sent or failed: mock rails settle in one step and a failed payment needs a rail error.
- Waiting counts compared for the owner, approver, clerk and accountant across the overview headline, sidebar, Inbox tab, Daily close KPI and panel.
- Rendered contrast walk over 28 pages per theme (including remit, evidence and onboarding in both languages). Keyboard tab order on the approval page, Daily close (desktop and phone) and overview.

- **Dense tables.** Accounting at 1440: the Vendor column is squeezed to three lines ("Linh / Family / Homestay") while Account code truncates ("310 Cost of Goods Sold: Co..."). Readable, but widen Vendor and let Account code truncate sooner.
- **Inbox default per role.** Owner, approver and clerk land on Waiting for you (6, 4, 3); the accountant (0) lands on All 37. No empty or confusing view. The Needs action tab is still a separate, larger number (6 for the approver, including 2 blocked requests that belong to the clerk).
- **Daily close.** The new Waiting on someone else panel works (clerk: Dao, Linh, Ana awaiting an approver; accountant: all four). Two things a time-pressured owner will notice: the same blocked requests appear in Needs a look and again in Still waiting on you (documented), and the KPI sub-line "your next step, as in the sidebar" is the interface explaining its own bookkeeping. Folding works for New vendor and approved-over-warning lines; identical blocked duplicates (I created three) are not folded, which is acceptable.
- **Toasts and errors.** Source reviewed: errors are icon plus "Error:" plus `role="alert"`. Onboarding errors sit under each field with a focused summary. `error.tsx` is still untriggerable, as before.
- **401 page.** Unchanged and good: HTML, name, claim, where the login is, `WWW-Authenticate` header intact.
- **Dark mode.** Daily close (desktop and phone), approval band with chip, overview and onboarding all read correctly; no loss of contrast.
- **Anything unfinished.** Nothing that looks like a stub. The run page is plain but complete.

## Overclaim sweep

Sentences that still say "clean" or "cleared": the overview and Settings autonomy copy ("The agent sends clean payments up to USD 500 each", "Clean requests within your caps are sent by the agent"), the History line "Policy cleared it to pay", the audit row "Cleared it to pay", and the live feed "passed the rules, nobody needed to approve". All of them are about what the policy engine decided, and the feed and demo guide now say so ("The rules pass it on their own ... the recorded Claude reading shows a possible duplicate ... the card says so before the button"). I count them as acceptable, with one suggestion: "clean" in the autonomy copy means "the rules pass", and a one-word change ("requests the rules pass") would close the gap with the reader. The timeline, the activity feed, the audit log, the Policy panel ("Every rule passed. The agent's concerns are not rules; read them under What the agent read."), the vendor evidence page and the remittance page make no pass claim.

## New findings

**NEW-8 (Should-fix). The Ready to pay band stays green, with a check icon, over open reader concerns.** `/obligations/<Maria JOB-HG4D-0921>` and Ana once approved. The words are right ("Rules passed, but the recorded Claude run raised 2 concerns") and there is a "2 reader concerns" chip, but the band is the green settled tone with a green check icon and a green chip, so at a glance it still reads "all good". On the awaiting band the same concern is amber. Fix: when the statement is not clear, use the attention tone, a warning icon and an amber chip on the Ready to pay band, and keep the green for a clear statement.

**NEW-9 (Should-fix, highest priority). The overview's autopilot countdown shows no concern.** `/` with autopilot on: "Maria Santos USD 180.00. Autopilot sends it when the timer runs out. Cancel keeps it for a person. sends in 8:37 [Cancel]", while the request page says the recorded run raised two concerns and flagged a possible duplicate. The countdown is the one place a person can stop an automatic payment, and it carries less than the page. Fix: show the same chip and one-clause concern ("2 reader concerns: possible duplicate") on the countdown row and in the Daily close "About to go out" row.

**NEW-10 (Should-fix). The payment run page does not show reader concerns.** `/runs/<run>` as approver: Ana Reyes JOB-PAL3D-0927 reads "Ready to pay" with no note while Kalaw (a rule warning) gets "Has a warning; release leaves it for its own page". "Release USD 1,178.40" and its confirmation ("Yes, release USD 1,178.40") then include a request whose recorded reading flagged a possible duplicate. The run page makes no pass claim, but it is the batch money click. Fix: show the same reader-concern note and chip on run lines, and let release skip them the way it skips rule warnings, or name them in the confirmation.

**NEW-11 (Nice-to-have). Remit page in Vietnamese keeps an English title, skip link and date**; the evidence page is English for a Vietnamese vendor (declared).

**NEW-12 (Nice-to-have). Daily close repeats blocked requests across two panels for the owner.** Fix: omit from Still waiting on you any item already shown in Needs a look, or tag it "also above".

**NEW-7 stays open** (see table).

## Fixer decisions, re-checked

- **Needs action versus Waiting for you.** The new label is fine on the overview ("4 requests wait on you. 5 need action in all.") because the sentence defines it. As a bare tab ("Waiting for you 4 | All 37 | Needs action 6") it is abstract: a real approver will not know that "Needs action" includes the clerk's blocked requests. Suggestion: "Needs a person" or "Open, any role", and keep the sentence on the overview.
- **Blocked requests in two Daily close panels.** Documented and defensible (what went wrong in the period versus what is still open), but see NEW-12.
- **Not re-stepping amber and red; Reject staying in Actions (now with a Reject link).** Both remain acceptable. The Reject link closes the gap I raised.
- **Trust sentence and example placeholders.** Unchanged and acceptable.

## Ratings

- Overall design: **8.5 / 10** (was 8.0). A coherent, honest, accessible system with the earlier contradiction closed; what remains is tone and omission at the edges of the reader-concern story.
- Against the hackathon's design criterion: **9.0 / 10** (was 8.5). A judge sees a distinctive device (the check line), a first screen that leads with a decision, a decision band that says what it knows and what it does not, a phone that never scrolls sideways, and a Vietnamese vendor page that stays Vietnamese in its errors.
