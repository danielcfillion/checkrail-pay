# Human action queue

Tasks only Daniel can do: they need a personal login, a signature, a payment method, a phone or a face. The AI builder lane works on everything else in parallel. Business decisions only Daniel can make (pricing, lead prize, SCF go or no-go, entity, pilots, domain, lawyer) are in `docs/FOUNDER_DECISIONS.md` (F1 to F8) and are not repeated here. Work the queue top to bottom. When a task is done, change its status and paste what it says to return into the chat so the builder lane can continue.

Status values: Not started, In progress, Done, Blocked.

## Notes

- The single highest-value minute in the whole project: obligation 3 paying through the real PayPal sandbox (H1) with the payout batch id visible in the sandbox dashboard. Two judges' worth of score depends on it. Do H1 first.
- PayPal Payouts on a live (non-sandbox) account is an approved feature for business accounts and may not be available to Vietnam-registered senders. The hackathon only needs the sandbox. Do not apply for live Payouts until an entity that can own it is decided.
- Before any real money: set `CHECKRAIL_USERS` with named logins (live mode refuses to open without them), use a separate read-only PayPal app for the agent's tools, and read "Known gaps" under Safety in the README.
- If a Stellar attempt shows status unknown, do not retry blindly. Check the treasury account on a block explorer first; the app refuses a second payment while an earlier attempt is unresolved.

- Order now: H3, then H14, then H13 and H6 (the video), then H9. H2 and H15 only if F3 is a go.
- H6 benefits from everything else being done first. If time runs short, record in mock mode and say so on screen; the sidebar footer already shows the mode.
- Keep every secret in `.env` only. The repo ignores `.env*`. Never paste a secret into chat, a doc or a commit message.
